# Where it is fought

Boxing is cheap. A gym, hand wraps and a bag are enough to start, and for a
century the sport has been strongest in communities that other Olympic sports
do not reach.

Cuba is the outstanding case. Its state amateur system produced Olympic
champions for decades in which its boxers could not turn professional.
Teófilo Stevenson won the Olympic heavyweight title three times and never
fought for money.

Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Great Britain, the United States, Russia, Ukraine,
Ireland, Mexico and Thailand all produce international boxers, from very
different systems. Ireland shows what a small country can do. A network of
city clubs with almost no funding, in a population of five million, has
brought sustained Olympic success. Katie Taylor won the lightweight title at
the 2012 Games.

The collapse of the federation did not fall evenly across this map. The IOC
wrote the qualification system for the 2020 and 2024 tournaments itself.
Countries whose route to the Games ran through the federation's continental
events had to adapt at short notice to a structure designed somewhere else.
