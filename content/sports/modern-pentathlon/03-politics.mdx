# A sport rebuilt to stay in the Games

For two decades modern pentathlon has been close to losing its Olympic place.
It is expensive to stage and hard to broadcast. Few people take part
worldwide, and it has no professional circuit and no audience of its own. The
International Olympic Committee reviews sports for cost, worldwide spread,
audience and appeal to the young. Pentathlon has been near the bottom on
these and has been named again and again as a candidate for removal.

Its rule changes make sense as answers to that review.

- [In 2009 shooting and running were merged](#uipm-2009-combined-event) into
  one event with a <Term id="handicap-start">handicap start</Term>. Before
  that, the winner was whoever had the most points once five scores were
  added up. Now the leader is the person in front, and there is a finish line
  to point a camera at.
- [In 2011 laser pistols](#uipm-2011-laser-pistols) removed the need for a
  shooting range.
- [In 2024 the Olympic competition became a final of ninety
  minutes](#uipm-2024-laser-run-format) in one place, short enough for one
  broadcast.
- [From 2028 riding is gone](#uipm-2022-riding-removed). It was the most
  expensive part, and the part that drew objections about animal welfare.

## The athletes say no

The decision on riding, taken in 2022, turned a strategy into a crisis. A
large part of the sport's own athletes opposed it in public. They said the
consultation was inadequate and that the reasons given were not the real
ones. They formed an organisation, Pentathlon United, to fight the decision.
The federation went ahead.

The federation's reasons and the athletes' objection are both on record. The
plain fact is that a sport removed one of its founding disciplines against
the stated wishes of many of the people who compete in it.
