# Who makes the calls

Modern pentathlon has almost no officials of its own. Each discipline brings
the judges and the instruments of the sport it comes from.

[Fencing](/sports/fencing/) is judged by fencing's rules, and electric
apparatus registers each hit. The swim is timed by touchpads on the pool
wall. <Term id="show-jumping">Show jumping</Term> is judged by its own
faults, such as a <Term id="refusal">refusal</Term> or a
<Term id="knockdown">knockdown</Term>. The
<Term id="laser-run">laser run</Term> is timed, and its targets register the
shots electronically.

<Photo id="modern-pentathlon-epee" />

What pentathlon adds is the arithmetic. The
<Term id="points-table">points table</Term> does the work a referee does in
other sports. Nobody applies it during the competition. The federation
decides in advance how many points a second of swimming is worth against a
fencing hit.

The laser made the shooting simple to run. It once needed a range, a way to
score the targets and a safety officer. Now it needs a sensor.
[Taekwondo](/sports/taekwondo/) did something similar with electronic body
protectors, and [archery](/sports/archery/) with optical scoring.

<Photo id="modern-pentathlon-sensor-targets" />

Riding could never be handled this way. No sensor can say whether a horse
refused a fence because of the horse or because of the rider. The sport
ended that question by removing the horse.
