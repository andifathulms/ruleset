# The pistol and the horse

The two great changes in the equipment of modern pentathlon were both about
the cost of holding a competition. Neither was about performance.

## The pistol

[In 2011 laser pistols replaced air pistols](#uipm-2011-laser-pistols). The
change sounds small. It took the projectile out of the sport. With it went
the range, the backstop behind the targets, the firearms licence, and the
problem of carrying ammunition across borders. A
<Term id="laser-pistol">laser pistol</Term> can be shot in a school sports
hall.

<Photo id="modern-pentathlon-laser-pistols" />

The shooting did not get easier. The trigger, the hold and the timing are the
same. What changed is who can afford to host a competition and who may
legally practise, and the federation said plainly that this was the purpose.

<Photo id="modern-pentathlon-laser-shooting" />

## The horse

The organiser provides the horses. Each athlete draws one by lot and has
twenty minutes with an animal they have never ridden, then jumps a course on
it. That arrangement gave the riding its drama. It also gave it its
unfairness and its cost, and in the end it was the reason the discipline was
removed.

<Photo id="modern-pentathlon-drawn-horse" />

[From 2028 the fifth discipline is obstacle
racing](#uipm-2022-riding-removed).
<Term id="obstacle-racing">Obstacle racing</Term> needs a frame and a field,
and no living participant except the athlete. It was the cheapest choice
available, and the federation has not denied that cost was among its reasons.

The <Term id="epee">épée</Term> has not changed.
