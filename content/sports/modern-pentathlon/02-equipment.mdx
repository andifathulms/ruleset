# Equipment evolution

Two equipment changes, and both were about staging cost rather than
performance — which makes modern pentathlon the only sport on this site whose
equipment history is really a venue history.

**The pistol, 2011.** Air pistols were replaced by laser pistols. This sounds
minor and is not: it removed the projectile from the sport. With it went the
range, the backstop, the safety perimeter, the firearms licensing, and the
problem of moving ammunition across borders for an international competition.
A discipline that had required a specialist facility became one that could be
staged in a sports hall.

Nothing about the shooting got easier. The trigger, the hold and the timing are
the same. What changed is who can afford to host it and who can legally
practise it, and the federation was explicit that this was the point.

**The horse, from 2028.** Not an equipment change in any normal sense, and the
largest one here. A horse is provided by the organiser, drawn by lot, and the
competitor has twenty minutes with an animal they have never ridden before
jumping a course on it. That arrangement is the source of the discipline's
drama, its unfairness, its cost and, ultimately, its removal.

Obstacle racing needs a frame, a field and no living participant other than the
athlete. It is the cheapest fifth discipline the sport could have chosen, and
the federation has not disputed that this was among the reasons.

The épée has not changed.
