# What is still argued about

**Whether the removal of riding was legitimate.** The process itself is
disputed, apart from the result. Many athletes said the consultation was
inadequate and the stated reasons were not the real ones. The federation went
ahead, and Pentathlon United still opposes the decision.

**Whether it is still modern pentathlon.** De Coubertin's sport had a stated
purpose, and the purpose needed a horse. The federation's position is that a
sport is what it is today. Its critics answer that this sport had a
definition and gave it up.

**Whether any of it will save the sport.** Every change was meant to keep
modern pentathlon in the Olympic Games, and its place is still under review.
If it is removed anyway, the sport will have taken itself apart for nothing.
The federation has accepted that risk, and its critics have named it.

**The points tables.** The federation still writes them, and a change can
still decide who wins. They are revised with less attention than a change to
a playing rule would get.

**The rules of obstacle racing.** The new discipline has no settled
international standard of its own. Its rulebook is being written while the
discipline arrives.
