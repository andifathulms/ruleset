# What is contested now

Recorded as unsettled, and this sport has more genuinely open questions than
any other here.

**Whether the removal of riding was legitimate.** Not whether it was wise — the
process itself is contested. A substantial part of the athlete body argued the
consultation was inadequate and that the stated reasons were not the operative
ones. The federation proceeded. Pentathlon United continues to oppose it. This
is the only rule change on the site actively disputed by the people it applies
to, in public, at the time of writing.

**Whether it is still modern pentathlon.** De Coubertin's sport had a stated
purpose that required a horse. Without one, the remaining disciplines are a
coherent test and not an account of anything. The federation's position is that
a sport is what it is now rather than what it was designed as; the counter-
position is that this one had a definition and has abandoned it.

**Whether any of it will save the sport.** Every change here has been aimed at
keeping modern pentathlon on the Olympic programme, and its position remains
under review. If it is removed after all this, the sport will have dismantled
itself for nothing — which is a risk the federation has accepted and its
critics have named.

**The scoring tables.** Still written by the federation, still able to change
who wins, and still revised without the scrutiny a change to a playing rule
would attract.

**Obstacle racing's own rules.** The replacement discipline has no settled
international competition standard of its own, so the sport is importing a
component whose rulebook is being written as it arrives.
