# Where it is contested

Few people in the world take part in modern pentathlon, and the map of the
sport is a map of who can pay for four facilities.

Hungary, Russia, Italy, Great Britain, Germany, France, Poland, South Korea,
China, Egypt and Mexico produce most international results. Each has a
national programme that can put an athlete in a pool, a fencing hall, a
shooting range and an equestrian centre, and provide a coach in every one.

Hungary stands apart. It has treated modern pentathlon as a national sport
for most of a century, with a system of clubs that exists nowhere else.

Riding set the limit. Horses are expensive, and in most countries only a
narrow part of society can reach them. A discipline that needs an equestrian
centre needs a certain kind of country. This is one of the federation's
stated reasons for the change to
<Term id="obstacle-racing">obstacle racing</Term>.

Whether the change will widen the map is an open question. The other
disciplines still need a pool, a fencing hall and a range.
