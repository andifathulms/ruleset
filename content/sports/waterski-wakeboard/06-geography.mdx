# Warm, still water

Tournament water skiing needs calm water, warm weather and the money to put a
boat on a lake.

The United States invented the sport and is still its strongest nation.
Florida is a training centre for skiers from all over the world. It has warm
water all year, lakes built for skiing, and more coaches and makers of
equipment than anywhere else. Canada, Australia and New Zealand have the same
kind of scene.

In Europe, France, Great Britain, Italy, Belgium, Switzerland, Germany and
Russia all compete strongly. Latin America has a growing presence.

## The private lake

Much top-level skiing takes place on water that was built for it. A ski lake
is narrow and sheltered, and it is shaped so that the boat's wash dies away
quickly. Many are privately owned. The sport gathers where land, water and
spare income are found together.

## The cable park

Cable parks spread differently. One <Term id="cable">cable</Term> tows many
riders at once, and a rider pays for a session and needs no boat. Europe has
the most cable parks, and Germany, where the system was invented, has a large
cable scene. Cable parks have also brought the sport to the Middle East and
Southeast Asia, where few people could have paid for boats.
