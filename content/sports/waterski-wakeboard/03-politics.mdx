# Twice at the Olympic door

Water skiing has been seen at the Olympic Games once and has never won a
medal there. In 1972 it was [a demonstration sport at
Munich](#waterski-1972-olympic-demonstration). Demonstration sports were shown
at the Games without being part of the programme, and the Olympic movement
stopped holding them after 1992.

The second chance came through wakeboarding. It was young, it used a board,
and it looked good on screen. The federation put it forward for the 2020
Games, and [it was shortlisted](#waterski-2013-wakeboard-shortlist). It was
not chosen. [Skateboarding](/sports/skateboarding/) and
[surfing](/sports/surfing/), two other board sports, were both added to those
Games.

Other federations have taken in a discipline from street or board culture and
won an Olympic place with it. The [roller sports](/sports/roller-sports/) body
did it with skateboarding, and the [dance sport](/sports/dancesport/)
federation did it, for a short time, with [breaking](/sports/breaking/). The
water ski federation tried the same thing with wakeboarding and it did not
work.

## The cost of a boat

The federation's other long problem is money. Towing behind a boat needs a
boat, a driver, fuel and a stretch of water for each competitor. That limits
how many people can take part. Cable parks are the answer the sport has found.
One cable tows many riders at once, and cable riding is growing faster than
the boat disciplines.
