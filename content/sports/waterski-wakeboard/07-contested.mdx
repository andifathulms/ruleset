# What is still argued about

**Cable or boat.** Cable riding is cheaper, easier to reach and growing
faster. It also does without the boat, which the federation was founded
around. Which of the two is the sport's future is an open question. The
numbers of people taking part point one way and the tournament tradition
points the other.

**The driver.** Electronic speed control took the speed out of the driver's
hands. The line is still steered by a person, and a driver's error can still
cancel a <Term id="pass">pass</Term>. No further fix has been adopted.

**Comparing eras.** Boats and wakes have improved for eighty years and made
slalom passes easier. The scoring has not changed to match. The sport has
never said what that does to its own history.
[Bowling](/sports/bowling/) has the same difficulty with its modern balls.

**The Olympic Games.** Water skiing was a demonstration in 1972. Wakeboarding
was shortlisted for 2020 and passed over. Whether any route is left, and which
discipline would take it, is unknown.

**Jump.** Jump is the most dangerous discipline and has a history of serious
injuries. Top jumpers now fly so far that the landing is the hard part. The
ramp, the boat speed and the equipment have all been adjusted, and the
distances keep growing.
