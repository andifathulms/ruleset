# The ball that flew too far

The longest argument in golf is about distance, and it ran for about thirty
years before anything was decided.

One side said that longer and longer drives were making historic courses too
short to test the best players. Courses had been lengthened for decades, and
each extension cost land, money and water. The governing bodies, this side
said, had a duty to act.

The other side said distance was no problem at all. Manufacturers and players
had invested in the game as it was. A shorter ball would punish everyone for
what a few hundred professionals could do.

## The decision

In December 2023 the R&A and the USGA chose. [They changed the conditions
under which balls are tested, so that a legal ball must fly
shorter](#golf-2028-ball-rollback). The change applies to elite competition
from 2028 and to all golfers from 2030. Manufacturers and a large part of the
professional game opposed it.

Most equipment rules stop a new advantage early. This one takes back distance
that has been in the game for decades.

## One game or two

The first proposal would have applied only to elite competition and left
everyday golfers alone. The governing bodies withdrew it. Golf claims that
everyone plays the same game under the same rules, from a club competition to
a major championship. Two sets of ball rules would have ended that claim, and
they chose to change the ball for everybody.
