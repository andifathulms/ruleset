# What is still argued about

**The shorter ball.** It was decided in 2023 and takes effect from 2028, and
the argument has not stopped. Distances and scores from before the change will
describe a different ball.

**One set of rules.** The governing bodies kept a single game for everyone
this time. The claim will be tested again at the next equipment question, and
the answer may be different.

**Pace of play.** The 2019 rewrite tried to speed the game up, and a round
still takes about four hours. That keeps people away. Every plan to shorten it
meets the same fact: a round has always been eighteen holes.

**The four majors.** Golf's four major championships are run by four separate
organisations, and two of those own the Rules. No single body governs all
four. Each sets its own conditions and its own
<Term id="play-off">play-off</Term> format, and nobody has tried to make them
agree.
