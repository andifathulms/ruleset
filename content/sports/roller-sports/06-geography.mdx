# Three maps

Each discipline is strong in different countries, and no country is strong in
all three.

## Rink hockey

<Term id="rink-hockey">Rink hockey</Term> belongs to southern Europe and South
America. Portugal, Spain, Italy and Argentina are the powers. They have
professional or semi-professional leagues, many clubs and real crowds. In
Portugal and in Catalonia it is a mainstream sport. France, Switzerland,
Germany, Chile and Brazil play it too. So do Angola and Mozambique, where it
arrived through Portuguese colonial rule.

## Speed

Colombia has dominated speed skating for years, more completely than almost
any nation dominates any sport. Italy, Chinese Taipei, South Korea, Belgium,
France, Spain, the United States and New Zealand follow.

## Artistic

Artistic skating is strongest in Italy, Germany, Spain, Argentina, Colombia,
the United States and Chinese Taipei.

## Where there was no ice

These maps hardly overlap with the maps of the ice sports. Colombia and
Portugal are not known for figure skating or ice speed skating. The roller
disciplines took root in the places that had no ice.

A world championship in roller sports is in effect three championships,
attended by three different groups of nations.
