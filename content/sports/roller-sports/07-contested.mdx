# What is still argued about

**What the merger gave the older disciplines.** The federation's Olympic
recognition won a place for skateboarding four years after it joined. Whether
artistic skating, speed skating or rink hockey gained anything is asked inside
the federation, and nobody has a clear answer.

**Living beside the ice sports.** Artistic and speed skating each have a twin
in the Winter Games. No argument for adding the wheeled versions has worked so
far.

**Rink hockey's case.** Rink hockey has the best claim of the three. It has no
close twin on the ice, it has professional leagues, and it is a mainstream
sport in several countries. Its one Olympic appearance was the 1992
demonstration.

**Judging artistic skating.** The worries came with the method from figure
skating: bloc voting, and how to separate two clean routines of unequal
difficulty.

**Where the athletes go.** Rink hockey offers a professional career in three
countries. Artistic and speed skating offer almost none. A world champion in
either has reached the end of the road.
