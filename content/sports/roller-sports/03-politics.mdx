# The discipline that got in

No roller skating discipline has ever been on the Olympic programme. The
federation was founded in 1924 and governed artistic skating, speed skating
and rink hockey for nearly a century without placing any of them there.

## Barcelona, 1992

Rink hockey came closest. In Spain it is a mainstream sport with a
professional league, and when the Games went to Barcelona in 1992 [it was
played there as a demonstration sport](#roller-1992-olympic-demonstration). A
demonstration sport was shown at the Games without being part of the
programme. After Barcelona the Olympic movement stopped holding them. Rink
hockey has not been back.

## The twin on the ice

The obstacle is simple. Artistic roller skating is figure skating on wheels.
Roller speed skating is speed skating without ice. Both ice sports are already
in the Winter Games, so anyone choosing new sports can say that the Games hold
those contests already.

## 2017

In 2017 the roller federation [merged with the international skateboarding
body](#roller-2017-world-skate) and became World Skate.
[Skateboarding](/sports/skateboarding/) needed a recognised federation to
enter the Games, and the roller federation was recognised. Four years later
skateboarding was at the Tokyo Games.

<Photo id="roller-sports-world-skate-games-2024" />

The recognition had been built over ninety years by artistic skaters, speed
skaters and hockey players. It won an Olympic place for the discipline that
had just joined, and skateboarding was the one discipline with no twin on the
ice.

Some skateboarders argued that a roller skating federation should not govern
them. The same pattern appeared in [dance sport](/sports/dancesport/), where a
ballroom federation took [breaking](/sports/breaking/) to the 2024 Games while
its own dances stayed outside.
