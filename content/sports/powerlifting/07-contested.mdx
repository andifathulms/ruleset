# What is still argued about

**Whose records are the records.** Every federation claims the answer and none
can enforce it. No single line of world records exists for that reason.

**Raw and equipped.** Raw lifting has most of the growth, most of the new
lifters and most of the attention. Equipped lifters answer that theirs is a
separate discipline with decades of technique behind it. Whether one
federation should keep two full record books is an open question.

**Squat depth.** The standard is spoken, judged by eye, and applied unevenly
between federations and sometimes within one. It could be measured by
instrument, and it is not.

**Testing.** The split that shaped the sport has never healed, and nothing
exists that could heal it. Untested federations are a large part of
powerlifting.

**The Olympic Games.** The International Powerlifting Federation has a World
Games place and international recognition. Many inside the sport doubt it can
go further while its own record book is disputed and the public confuses it
with [weightlifting](/sports/weightlifting/).

**The points formula.** Comparing a 59 kg lifter with a 120 kg lifter takes a
<Term id="coefficient">points coefficient</Term>. The formula has been revised
more than once, and each version changes who would have won awards handed out
years before.
