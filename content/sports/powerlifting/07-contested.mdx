# What is contested now

Recorded as unsettled.

**Which federation's records are the records.** Every federation asserts an
answer and none can enforce it. This site takes no position and records that
the disagreement is the reason no unified progression exists.

**Whether raw and equipped should both continue.** Raw has most of the growth,
most of the new competitors and most of the visibility. Equipped lifters argue
theirs is a distinct discipline with its own decades of technique. Whether a
federation should maintain two full record books is a live question.

**Squat depth.** The standard is verbal, judged by eye, applied inconsistently
between federations and sometimes within them, and could be instrumented. That
it has not been is the sport's most conspicuous refusal.

**Testing.** The split that created the sport's structure has never healed and
there is no mechanism by which it could. An untested federation is not a fringe
concern here; it is a substantial part of the sport.

**Olympic ambition.** The IPF holds a World Games place and international
recognition. Whether inclusion is realistic for a sport whose own record book
is contested, and which the public routinely confuses with the weightlifting
already on the programme, is doubted inside the sport as much as outside it.

**The coefficient.** How to compare a 59 kg lifter with a 120 kg one is a
formula, the formula keeps changing, and each version rewrites who won awards
that were handed out years ago.
