# When the kit began to lift

A belt and a pair of wraps help a lifter stay safe. A bench shirt helps lift
the bar. The story of powerlifting equipment is the move from the first kind
to the second.

<Photo id="powerlifting-belt-and-wraps" />

Belts and wraps came first, and nobody argued about them. A belt raises the
pressure inside the abdomen. Knee wraps and wrist wraps steady a joint.

## The suit and the shirt

In the late 1970s and the 1980s came the squat suit and the
<Term id="bench-shirt">bench shirt</Term>. They were made of dense polyester,
and later of canvas, and they were cut smaller than the lifter on purpose.

<Photo id="powerlifting-bench-shirt" />

A photograph from Lajes Field in the Azores in 2009 shows what that means. Two
helpers stand behind a lifter and drag a tight black shirt down over his back.
He cannot put it on alone.

These garments store elastic energy at the bottom of the lift and give it back
on the way up. An <Term id="equipped">equipped</Term> lift is a different
mechanical event from a <Term id="raw">raw</Term> one. A lifter's bench press
in a shirt can be far heavier than the same lifter's bench press without one.
Gear made of several layers stitched together pushed the gap wider still.
[The federation permitted the equipment and then regulated
it](#pl-equipped-lifting).

<Photo id="powerlifting-helsinki-2001-bench" />

Federations disagreed about how much to allow. Some permit one layer, some
several, some none, and the three are close to being three separate sports.

## Two record books

The International Powerlifting Federation did not settle the argument. [In
2012 it added a classic division](#pl-2012-ipf-raw-division) for raw lifting,
with its own world championships and its own records. Raw lifting had been
growing fast outside the federation for years, and the federation was losing
that part of the sport to its rivals.

<Photos ids="powerlifting-classic-squat powerlifting-classic-squat-2" />

[Cycling](/sports/cycling/) did the same with the hour record: it separated
the conditions and kept both books. [Swimming](/sports/swimming/) went the
other way with its supersuits, banning the equipment and letting the marks
stand.

Equipped lifters make their own case. Using a bench shirt well takes years,
they say, and their division is a discipline in its own right. Nobody disputes
the physics. The dispute is over what the physics means.
