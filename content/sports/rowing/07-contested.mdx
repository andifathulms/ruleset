# What is still argued about

**Was it right to lose the lightweights?** The category gave athletes of
ordinary size a way into a sport that otherwise selects for height and weight.
It is gone from the Games. The federation points to the quota. Critics say the
sport also had a weight-cutting problem it did not want to defend, and that
the two reasons suited each other.

**Is beach sprint rowing?** Supporters say it frees the sport from a course
that few cities can build, and that this protects rowing's place at the Games.
Opponents say it is a different sport added under rowing's name to keep a
quota. Critics of [modern pentathlon](/sports/modern-pentathlon/) said much
the same when it brought in obstacle racing.

**Should lanes be drawn differently?** Crews still lose medals to a crosswind
and a bad lane. Proposals to seed the lanes another way, or to cancel results
rowed in bad conditions, come back again and again. They fail each time,
because any wind limit would be arbitrary.

**Should best times become records?** Some argue that rowers deserve a mark to
chase and that the sport is giving away a way to promote itself. The
federation has not moved.
