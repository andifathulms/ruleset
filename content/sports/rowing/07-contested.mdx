# What is contested now

Recorded as unsettled.

**Whether losing lightweight rowing was right.** The category existed to give
athletes of ordinary size a route into an elite sport that otherwise selects
hard for height and mass. It is now gone from the Olympic programme. The
federation cited quota arithmetic; critics note that the sport had a
weight-making problem it was uncomfortable defending, and that the two reasons
were convenient for each other.

Set against volleyball, which invented the libero to widen its physical door
and kept it, rowing closed its equivalent.

**Lane draws and conditions.** Still the sport's live competitive grievance.
A crosswind makes some lanes faster, championship medals have turned on the
draw, and the federation has no mechanism beyond re-racing in extreme cases.
Proposals to seed lanes differently or to abandon results in bad conditions
recur and go nowhere, because any threshold would be arbitrary.

**Beach sprint.** Arriving in 2028, and genuinely unsettled within the sport.
Supporters say it removes an infrastructure requirement that is strangling
rowing's Olympic position. Opponents say it is a different sport being added
under rowing's name to protect a quota, which is close to what modern
pentathlon's critics said about obstacle racing.

**Whether best times should become records.** Periodically proposed, on the
ground that athletes deserve a mark to chase and the sport denies itself a
promotional asset. The federation has not moved, and this site would note that
it is the only body here that has never had to clean up after a record it
should not have recognised.
