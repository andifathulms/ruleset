# The long road to the Olympics

Squash asked to join the Olympic Games for 2012, for 2016, for 2020 and for
2024. Each time the answer was no. Match length and the difficulty of showing
the game on television were among the reasons given.

## A sport rebuilt for the camera

The changes of those years point in one direction. Glass walls had already
made the game possible to film. [A lower tin in
2001](#wsf-2001-ball-and-tin) shortened the rallies. [Video review in
2019](#wsf-2019-video-review) dealt with the most visible weakness in the
refereeing. The largest change was to the scoring.

<Photos ids="squash-grand-central-2012 squash-grand-central-2019" />

Under the old <Term id="hand-in-hand-out">hand-in hand-out</Term> scoring, a
game went to nine points and only the server could score. A match could last
two hours or forty minutes, and nobody could say in advance which it would
be. No broadcaster could plan around that. [From 2004 a game goes to eleven
and every rally scores a point](#wsf-2004-point-a-rally). The professional
tour made the change before the amateur game did.

Not every player welcomed it. Some argued that the old scoring gave a player
who was behind a way back, and that the new scoring favoured power over
patience. The change also cut the sport off from its own past. Match
statistics from before 2004 are counted in a different unit and cannot be
compared with those after it.
[Volleyball](/sports/volleyball/) had made the same kind of change in 1999
and [table tennis](/sports/table-tennis/) in 2001, and
[badminton](/sports/badminton/) followed in 2006.

## The answer

[In October 2023 squash was added to the programme for
2028](#wsf-2023-olympic-inclusion), with a men's and a women's singles event.
Those Games will come twenty-four years after the scoring change.

Many sports change a rule and then find out what follows. Squash set out to
be admitted, changed itself over two decades, and was admitted.

## Who runs the game

The men's and women's professional tours are run as businesses apart from
the federation. Prize money for men and women became equal comparatively
early. The tours hold the broadcast contracts, which is why the professional
game moved to the new scoring first.
