# Where it is danced

Britain wrote the technique, and it still holds the competition that many
couples care about most. The Blackpool Dance Festival matters to them
whichever federation approves it.

<Photo id="dancesport-blackpool" />

The deepest competition is in Europe: Italy, Germany, Russia, Ukraine,
Poland, Moldova, Lithuania and Slovenia. Russian and Ukrainian couples have
dominated large parts of the sport for decades.

Asia is where dancesport has grown most. China has invested heavily and now
produces couples at the highest level, in the couples events and in
<Term id="formation">formation</Term>. Japan has a long competitive
tradition. Chinese Taipei, South Korea and Hong Kong all compete.

The United States has a large culture of social dancing in studios and a
comparatively small presence in international competition.

Most people who dance these dances never compete. Social ballroom and Latin
classes run in almost every country, and the great majority of the dancers
will never enter a competition or learn which federation would run it.
