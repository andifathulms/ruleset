# What is contested now

Recorded as unsettled.

**Whether the umbrella should hold.** The confederation unites codes that do
not play each other, for institutional rather than sporting reasons. It gives
the family a single voice and it means the recognised body governs no
competition. Whether that is a sensible structure or a bureaucratic fiction is
argued by the federations inside it.

**Paris 2024.** Pétanque was not selected for a Games held in the country where
it is a national game. The sport's own reading is that the additional-sport
mechanism is about youth audiences rather than about local resonance, and that
reading is hard to argue with. What to do next is genuinely open.

**Time limits.** Introduced for television in some formats and not adopted
generally. The sport has watched five others on this site rewrite their scoring
for schedulability and sever their own statistics; boules' bounded score means
it could adopt a clock without that cost, and it has still not done so
generally.

**The terrain.** Not standardising the ground is a deliberate choice that keeps
the sport playable anywhere and makes competition conditions uneven. Proposals
to prepare championship terrains more consistently recur.

**Where the growth is.** The sport's institutional weight is French and its
fastest-growing competitive nations are in Southeast Asia and Africa. Whether
the federation's centre of gravity should follow is a question about power
rather than about rules.
