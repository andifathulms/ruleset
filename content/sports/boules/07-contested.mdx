# What is still argued about

**A clock.** A game to thirteen points has no fixed length, which is hard on
anyone planning a broadcast. Some televised and championship formats now have
a time limit. The sport has not adopted one generally.

**The ground.** Leaving the <Term id="terrain">terrain</Term> rough keeps the
game playable anywhere. It also means that competition conditions are uneven.
Proposals to prepare championship pitches more consistently keep coming back.

<Photo id="boules-marseillaise-terrain" />

**Who runs it.** The sport's institutions are French. Its fastest-growing
competitive nations are in Southeast Asia and Africa. Whether the federation's
centre should move toward them is a question about power, and the rules do not
answer it.
