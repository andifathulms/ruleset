# One name for several games

Pétanque is one of several boules games. They share a world body and they do
not play each other.

Pétanque has its own international federation, [formed in
1958](#petanque-1958-fipjp). Boule lyonnaise, raffa and volo have their own.
All of them involve throwing a ball toward a smaller target ball. The throwing
action, the surface, the distances and the tactics all differ, and a champion
in one does not compete in another.

[The Confédération Mondiale des Sports de Boules was formed above them in
1985](#boules-1985-cmsb). Each federation was too small to be recognised
alone. Together they could present themselves as one sport, hold recognition
and seek places at multi-sport events. The body that holds the recognition
runs no competition of its own. Each game keeps its own rules and its own
championships. The federations inside it still argue about whether that is a
sensible arrangement.

## Paris 2024

The Olympic Games of 2024 were held in France, where pétanque is a national
pastime played by millions. A host city may propose extra sports for its own
Games, and [pétanque asked to be one of them](#petanque-2019-olympic-bid).

The organising committee chose
[breaking](/sports/breaking/), [skateboarding](/sports/skateboarding/),
[sport climbing](/sports/sport-climbing/) and [surfing](/sports/surfing/). The
criteria for that choice were about young and urban audiences. A French
committee applied them and left out the game most closely tied to France.

What pétanque should do next is undecided.
