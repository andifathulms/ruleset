# Where it is played

Pétanque is played most of all in France, and after that in countries that
France once ruled.

In France millions of people play. The game is found in town squares and on
club terrains, from the purely social to the professional, and it belongs
above all to the south.

<Photo id="boules-shooting-greoux" />

Much of the rest of the map follows the French language. Madagascar has been
one of the strongest pétanque nations in the world for decades. The country
has very little sporting infrastructure of any kind. The game arrived under
colonial rule and stayed. Cambodia, Laos, Vietnam, Tunisia, Morocco, Senegal
and Benin all play it widely.

Thailand is the exception. It took up the game on purpose, has invested
seriously in it since the 1980s, and is now a leading nation.

In Europe, Spain, Italy, Belgium and Switzerland compete. The other boules
games have their own maps: boule lyonnaise in France and Italy, raffa and volo
in Italy, Croatia and Slovenia.
