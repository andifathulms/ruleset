# What is still argued about

**Does annulling records achieve anything?** The sport has wiped its record
book twice, twenty-five years apart, for the same reason. Those in favour say
a record set under conditions the sport no longer accepts is a wall and no
longer a target. Those against say the 1993 reset did not fix the problem it
was aimed at, and that erasing numbers takes the place of governing.

**The place at the Games.** Weightlifting's Olympic status has been in real
doubt within the last decade. The penalties are still working through: fewer
places, fewer categories, reform imposed from outside.

**Categories that do not match.** The
<Term id="category">categories</Term> contested at the Games and the
categories in which world records are kept are now different sets. A lifter
can hold a world record in a division nobody can win an Olympic medal in.

**The press-out.** It is still the least consistent call in the sport, and
still judged by eye. Automating it has been proposed and never pursued.

**Making weight.** As in [judo](/sports/judo/) and
[boxing](/sports/boxing/), lifters compete at a weight fixed at the
<Term id="weigh-in">weigh-in</Term>. Severe dehydration before it is only
lightly regulated.
