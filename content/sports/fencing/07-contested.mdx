# What is contested now

Recorded as unsettled.

**Priority.** The last unmechanised judgement in the sport, decided by one
referee, frequently deciding medals, and disputed in almost exactly the terms
it was disputed in before electrification. Proposals to define it more tightly,
to expand video review, or to add a second referee circulate. None addresses
the underlying problem, which is that priority is a judgement about the shape
of an action rather than a fact about a moment.

**The timing constants.** The federation can reshape a weapon by adjusting a
number inside the scoring apparatus, as it did to sabre in 2005. There is a
real argument that decisions of that consequence should go through the same
scrutiny as a rule change, and currently they do not.

**Whether the three weapons should share a sport.** Épée, foil and sabre have
different rules, different constituencies and different national strengths, and
fencers specialise from an early age. Whether they are one sport with three
weapons or three sports with one federation is an old question that resurfaces
whenever the Olympic programme is reviewed.

**Watchability.** Fencing's central difficulty is that a spectator cannot tell
who scored without knowing the priority rule, and the sport has tried
transparent masks, better graphics and format changes without solving it. The
2005 sabre revision was partly an attempt at the same problem, by making the
weapon's exchanges shorter and clearer.
