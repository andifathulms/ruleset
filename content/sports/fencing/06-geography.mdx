# Where it is played

Italy, France, Hungary and Russia have led fencing for a century. Each has
fencing academies, military and civilian, that have run without a break for
generations. Germany, Poland, Ukraine and Romania add depth. Few sports have a
map this old or this steady.

## The newcomers

South Korea, China, Japan and the United States joined the leading nations
later, and each did it by decision.

South Korea is the clearest case. It saw that money could win medals in a
sport whose strength was gathered in a few European countries. It built a
programme around sabre and became a leading nation within about twenty years.
Britain did something similar in track [cycling](/sports/cycling/).

<Photo id="fencing-korea-2013" />

Egypt and several other African and Middle Eastern nations have produced world
medallists in recent years from very small numbers of fencers.

## Why the map changes slowly

A <Term id="piste">piste</Term> and a scoring machine can be bought. A
tradition of coaches who know how to teach right of way cannot be bought
quickly. That is why fencing has spread through planned national programmes,
and why it has spread so little otherwise.
