# What is contested now

Recorded as unsettled.

**Whether kayak cross belongs.** Four boats, contact permitted, first down
wins — inside a discipline built on individual timed descents. It makes slalom
watchable and it is a different kind of contest. Sport climbing is having
exactly this argument about speed, and neither federation has resolved it.

**The cost of whitewater.** Competitive slalom increasingly requires an
artificial course, which is an expensive engineered facility. The sport's
geography has contracted to countries that can build one, and no rule change
addresses it — the same problem hockey has with turf and swimming has with
pools.

**Parity paid for by deletion.** Women's canoe events arrived in 2021 by
removing men's events, because the athlete quota is fixed. That is the correct
outcome reached by a mechanism nobody defends, and it will recur every time the
programme is rebalanced.

**The fifty-second penalty.** Unambiguous, and severe enough that a single
missed gate ends a run outright. Whether a sport should punish one error that
heavily is argued after every major final, and the counter-argument — that any
graded alternative reintroduces a judge's assessment of severity — has held so
far.

**Two sports, one federation.** Sprint and slalom share a paddle, a quota and
almost nothing else, and every programme decision sets them against each other.
