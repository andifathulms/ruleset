# What is still argued about

**The set system.** Fifteen years on, archers still argue that a format in
which the better shooting can lose is the wrong way to decide an archery
match. The federation answers that the alternative was a sport nobody watched.
Both statements are true, and neither settles the other.

**The compound bow.** It is more accurate, it is more popular in several
countries, and it is still outside the Olympic programme. Admitting it means
replacing recurve events or enlarging a fixed quota, so the argument is with
the International Olympic Committee more than inside archery.

**The weather.** Archery has no wind limit and no rerun. Proposals for one or
the other come back whenever a final is decided in a gale, and the sport has
declined every time. The answer given is that shooting in the weather is the
sport. [Golf](/sports/golf/) says the same. [Athletics](/sports/athletics/)
chose the other way long ago.

**Finer scoring.** Electronic systems can measure where an arrow landed far
more finely than ten rings do. There are proposals from time to time to score
to a decimal, as [shooting](/sports/shooting/) does. It would separate archers
who now tie. It would also make new scores impossible to compare with old
ones.
