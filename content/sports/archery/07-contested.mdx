# What is contested now

Recorded as unsettled.

**The set system.** Fifteen years on, archers still argue that a format in
which the better shooting can lose is the wrong way to decide an archery match.
The federation's position is that the alternative was a sport nobody watched.
Both are true statements and they do not resolve each other, which is why this
is the clearest live example on the site of the broadcast-and-pacing cause
being weighed openly against the integrity of a measurement.

That the mixed team event, added in 2021, kept cumulative scoring is quiet
evidence that the federation itself does not regard the set system as
universally correct.

**The compound bow.** More accurate, more popular in several countries, and
still not on the Olympic programme. Admitting it means displacing recurve
events or expanding a fixed quota, so the argument is with the IOC rather than
within archery.

**Conditions.** Archery is the only measured sport here that does not
standardise its environment. There is no wind limit and no rerun, and
proposals to introduce either resurface whenever a final is decided in a gale.
The counter-argument is that shooting in weather is the sport, which is the
same argument golf makes and athletics rejected a century ago.

**Scoring precision.** Electronic systems can now measure arrow position far
more finely than the ten rings do, and there are periodic proposals to score to
a decimal as shooting does. It would separate archers who currently tie, and it
would sever the record a second time.
