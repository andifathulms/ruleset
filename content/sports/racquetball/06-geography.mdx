# Where it is played

Racquetball was invented in the United States, and its strongest players now
come from further south.

The United States drove the boom and is still a major power. It is also where
the decline is sharpest. From the 1980s onwards a generation of players lost
their courts while they still wanted to play.

## Mexico and Bolivia

Mexico is now arguably the strongest nation, with deep competition at home and
steady success abroad.

<Photo id="racquetball-san-luis-potosi" />

Bolivia is the surprise. It is a country of modest size and resources, and it
has produced world champions and beaten far larger nations again and again.
It did so with a concentrated national programme.

<Photos ids="racquetball-moscoso-2022 racquetball-bolivia-2015" />

## A sport of the Americas

Canada, Argentina, Costa Rica, Chile, Guatemala and Ecuador all compete
seriously. Racquetball has been part of the Pan American Games since 1995.
That place is its largest multi-sport stage and the most secure thing the
sport has.

<Photos ids="racquetball-pan-am-2015 racquetball-mixed-2022" />

Japan, South Korea, India and a few European countries play as well. The sport
never took root in Europe the way [squash](/sports/squash/) did.

## The courts

Everything depends on the buildings. Racquetball needs one particular kind of
closed court. Those courts were built for a boom that ended, and very few have
been built since. The sport spread because the courts were already there, and
it is held back now for the same reason.
