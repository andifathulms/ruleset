# What is still argued about

**Courts.** The stock of four-wall courts is shrinking in the country that
built them, and almost none are being built. No governing body can fix that
with a rule.

**Strategy or the ceiling?** [Squash](/sports/squash/) reached the Olympic
programme and racquetball did not. People inside the sport argue about why.
One view is that squash simply ran the better campaign. The other is that a
game with the ceiling in play is truly hard to film, whatever its federation
does.

**The hinder.** The <Term id="avoidable-hinder">avoidable hinder</Term> asks a
referee to judge in an instant what a player could have done. Each new
rulebook rewrites the wording, and the argument continues.

**Home or the world?** The sport's home is the Americas, and the countries
where it is growing are Latin American. The federation has little money. It
must choose between building on that base and continuing to chase a place on
every continent.
