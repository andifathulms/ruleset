# Where it is played

Flag football is played in more countries than American football, and its
centre is still the United States.

The United States has by far the most players, in schools, colleges, parks
and leagues. The National Football League's youth programmes promote the
game directly. Mexico is the strongest challenger and has been for years.
Mexican teams have won world championships, and the game was deep there
before any Olympic interest.

After those two, Panama, Austria, Italy, Israel, Japan and Canada have all
been competitive at world level. It is a scattered list, and the format
explains it. A country needs a patch of grass and twenty belts. The game grows
where somebody keen starts it.

2028 will have both tournaments, on the same format, with the same number of
teams.
