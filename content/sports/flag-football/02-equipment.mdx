# A belt and a ball

A flag football player wears no helmet and no pads. Contact is against the
rules, so there is nothing to pad against.

What a player does wear is a belt. It carries two
<Term id="flag">flags</Term>, one on each hip, fixed so that they come away
cleanly when pulled. The belt matters more than it looks. A flag that is
tucked into the shorts, held down, worn too tight or fixed at the wrong angle
can stop a defender from ending a play. Tampering with it is an offence.

<Photo id="flag-football-belt-and-flags" />

The ball is smaller than the ball of the eleven-a-side game. That matters
because nearly every play is a pass, and hands come in different sizes. Shirts
must be tucked in, and a uniform may have nothing on it that a flag can catch
on.

The game has no kicking, so it has no kicking tee and no goalposts.
