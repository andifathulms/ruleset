# What is still argued about

**Whether it stays at the Games.** A host city's proposal gives a sport one
Games and no more. [Karate](/sports/karate/) and
[breaking](/sports/breaking/) both came in that way and were dropped. The
federation is planning for a future it has not been promised.

**Whose sport got in.** The proposal came from Los Angeles with the backing of
an American professional league, for a game with most of its players in the
United States. IFAF says a worldwide amateur sport, with a world championship
since 2002, was recognised. Both accounts are on the record.

**Professional players.** Should players from the American professional
tackle league be allowed to play? The question has been discussed in public
and has not been settled. [Baseball](/sports/baseball/) met the same question
at the Olympics from the other side.

**Flag guarding.** The most common call in the game depends on telling a
deliberate shield from the ordinary swing of a runner's arm. No proposal to
make the call objective has gained ground.

**Whether the format is finished.** Five a side, the size of the field, the
no-run zones and the tiered <Term id="conversion">conversion</Term> are recent
choices. Some national federations already play to other rules, and the game
could change before its Olympic debut.
