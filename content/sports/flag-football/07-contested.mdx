# What is contested now

Recorded as unsettled.

**Whether it stays.** Inclusion for 2028 came through the host-city mechanism,
which grants one Games and no more. Karate was admitted that way and dropped;
breaking was admitted that way and dropped. Flag football's federation is
planning for a future that the decision it has does not guarantee.

**Whose interests brought it in.** The proposal came from Los Angeles with
American professional backing, for a sport whose largest playing base is
American. The counter-argument — a world championship since 2002, Mexican and
European teams winning it — is real. The dispute is over which fact is the
operative one.

**Professional participation.** Whether players from the American professional
tackle league should be eligible has been discussed publicly and not settled.
It is the same question baseball's Olympic status foundered on, arriving from
the opposite direction.

**Flag guarding.** The sport's most frequent call rests on distinguishing a
deliberate shield from the ordinary motion of a running person's arm. No
proposal to make it objective has gained ground.

**Whether the format is finished.** Five a side, fifty by twenty-five, no-run
zones and a tiered conversion are recent decisions, not inherited law. A sport
this young may well change the rules its Olympic debut is played under, and
some of its national federations play different ones already.
