# Three garments, two of them for gripping

A kurash wrestler wears three things, and the rules treat each one
differently.

The <Term id="yakhtak">yakhtak</Term> is the jacket. Most of the techniques of
kurash begin with a hold on it. One wrestler wears blue and the other green.

The <Term id="belbog">belbog</Term> is the belt, tied at the waist. It may be
gripped too.

The <Term id="ishton">ishton</Term> are the trousers, and they may not be
gripped. Nothing below the waist may be held, cloth or leg.

<Photo id="kurash-jacket-belt" />

There is no protective equipment and no footwear. The only other thing the
rules cover is the mat, and it matters less than in other grappling sports. A
bout that reaches the ground is stopped and restarted standing.
