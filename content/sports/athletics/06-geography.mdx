# Where it is played

Athletics has the widest participation of any sport on this site after football
and the most event-specific competitive geography of any sport anywhere. It is
not one map. It is forty.

Distance running is concentrated in the Rift Valley to a degree that has no
parallel in sport. Sprinting is concentrated in Jamaica, the United States and
West African diaspora populations. The throws are strongest in central and
eastern Europe. Race walking is dominated by a handful of nations that maintain
a judging and coaching culture for it. Pole vault requires a facility, and its
map looks like a map of facilities.

The honest thing to say about why is that the sources genuinely disagree.
Altitude, running to school, a coaching tradition, an economic incentive
structure, and selection effects from a very large training population have all
been proposed for the Rift Valley, and no single account is settled. This site
records that the concentration exists and declines to explain it, on the same
principle that governs `cause_primary` everywhere else — a plausible explanation
is not a sourced one.

What is worth recording is the rule consequence. Because athletics ranks by a
number rather than by a head-to-head result, a concentrated talent pool does not
produce dominance in the way it does in an invasion sport: it produces a deeper
qualifying field. And because entry to a Games is by qualifying standard as well
as by national quota, the sport's geography shows up in who is in the final
rather than in who is permitted to enter — which is a very different structure
from badminton, where the quota is the binding constraint.
