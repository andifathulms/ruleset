# Where it is played

Fistball lives in two places, on either side of an ocean, joined by
emigration.

The European heart is Germany, Austria and Switzerland, and Germany is the
strongest nation in both the men's and the women's game. Fistball there sits
inside the old gymnastics clubs, which give it halls, coaches and a long,
steady history. Italy and the Czech Republic play it too.

The South American heart is Brazil, Argentina and Chile. German-speaking
emigrants took the game there in the nineteenth and early twentieth centuries.
It is a tradition a century old, and Brazil and Argentina are among the best
teams in the world.

In 1997 the final of a club world cup put two of them together. Turnerschaft
Hannover from Germany played Sogipa from Porto Alegre, a Brazilian club founded in the same
tradition.

Namibia tells the same story again. Fistball arrived with German settlers
under colonial rule and stayed after that rule ended. The country still sends
teams to international events.

So the map of fistball is close to the map of German emigration. Every country
where it is strong received it from a German-speaking community.
