# What is contested now

Recorded as unsettled.

**Whether the sport can grow outside its diaspora.** Every country where
fistball is strong received it from German-speaking emigrants. The federation
has never established the sport somewhere with no such connection, and whether
it can is the question that determines everything else about its future.

**Whether rally scoring was worth it.** The change was made for
schedulability, which a sport of this size gets very little from. It cost the
comparability of its own statistics. The same trade was made by three larger
sports in the same period and is argued in all of them.

**Indoor or outdoor.** Two forms with separate world championships, played on
different field sizes in different seasons. Which is the real sport is not
settled, and the World Games contests the outdoor game.

**The tape height for women.** Set lower than the men's, as in most net sports,
and argued for the same reasons it is argued everywhere else.

**Whether to keep pursuing the Olympic programme.** Fistball has a genuine
century of continuous play, a real club structure and world championships on
two continents, and no plausible route onto a programme that selects on global
reach. Whether the campaign is worth the federation's limited resources, or
whether the World Games is simply the right home, is a live internal question —
and one that several sports on this site are having at the same time.
