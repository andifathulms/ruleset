# What is contested now

Recorded as unsettled.

**Whether it is a sport.** Still genuinely contested, and in the United States
still legally consequential, because the answer affects gender-equity
obligations in educational funding. Olympic recognition is a strong argument
and it has not settled the question in the places where it matters.

**The injury rate.** The safety code restricts and does not eliminate. A sport
that throws people into the air with no protective equipment has a floor on its
risk, and whether the current restrictions sit in the right place is argued
after every serious injury.

**Commercial concentration.** Much of the American competitive circuit and its
attendant apparel and camp business sits with a single company, and antitrust
litigation has been brought. No other sport covered here has its competitive
infrastructure owned this way.

**Which division represents the sport.** Cheer and Performance Cheer are
separately judged, separately populated, and would present very differently on
any programme.

**Olympic prospects.** Full recognition since 2021, a World Games place, and a
participation base concentrated in one country whose competitive structure is
commercial. The federation is making the case; nothing about the obstacles has
changed.

**Safeguarding.** A young, majority-female, coach-dependent participation base
has produced abuse cases and continues to demand systems that the sport is
still building.
