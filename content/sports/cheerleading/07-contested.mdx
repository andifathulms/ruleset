# What is still argued about

**The injury rate.** The safety code limits the danger and cannot remove it.
A sport that throws people into the air without protective equipment will
always carry some risk. After every serious injury there is an argument about
whether the limits are in the right place.

**Whether it is a sport.** Olympic recognition is a strong argument that it
is. It has not settled the matter in the United States, where the answer
changes what schools and colleges owe under gender-equity rules.

**Which division stands for the sport.** <Term id="cheer">Cheer</Term> and
<Term id="performance-cheer">Performance Cheer</Term> share a federation and
little else. They are judged separately and performed by different athletes,
and they would look very different on any programme.

**The Olympic Games.** The federation has had full recognition since 2021 and
is making its case for a place. Most participants are still in one country,
and the competitions there are run commercially.

**Safeguarding.** The athletes are young and mostly female, and they depend
on their coaches. There have been abuse cases, and the sport is still
building the systems to prevent them.
