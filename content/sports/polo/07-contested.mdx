# What is still argued about

**Cloning.** Copies of one outstanding mare have played and won at the top of
the game, and no governing body has ruled on it. The question underneath is
what a polo <Term id="pony">pony</Term> is. If it is a competitor, cloning is
a question of eligibility. If it is equipment, cloning is manufacturing. Polo
has never had to decide, and the practice keeps growing.

**Horse welfare.** The hard work, the number of animals a career uses up and
the standard of veterinary care are all under question. The twenty-one deaths
in Florida in 2009 are still the case people cite.

**The world championship.** The federation caps team handicaps to keep its
championship open, and so shuts out the best players. People inside the sport
argue about what a world title is worth when the best in the world cannot
play for it.

**Ratings.** Handicaps are set by committee, and they decide whether a
professional is hired and for how much. Disputes are constant.

**Cost.** Horses will never be cheap. Clubs that hire out ponies by the chukka
and polo schools are the only ways in for most people. The handicap makes a
beginner welcome in a real match. It does nothing to help anyone reach the
field.

**The Olympics.** The federation holds Olympic recognition. The problem of
moving so many horses is the same as it was in 1936.
