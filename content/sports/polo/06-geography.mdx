# Argentina, and everyone who hires Argentines

Argentina leads polo more completely than most countries lead any sport. It
has the deepest club system and the breeding industry that supplies the
world. Almost every living player with a ten-goal
<Term id="handicap">handicap</Term> is Argentine. Argentine professionals are
hired by teams around the world and go home for their own season.

## Palermo

The top of the sport is a club tournament. The Argentine Open, played at
Palermo in Buenos Aires, is contested at the highest team handicaps anywhere,
and every leading player wants to win it.

<Photos ids="polo-palermo-2014 polo-windsor-2017" />

Great Britain and the United States hold the other two important high-goal
seasons. All three are paid for by <Term id="patron">patrons</Term>: amateurs
who fund a team and play in it beside the professionals they hire. The
British and American seasons bring in Argentine players and Argentine horses
to fill their teams.

## A world championship without the best players

The Federation of International Polo was formed in 1982 and gained Olympic
recognition in 1998. It runs a world championship with a cap on each team's
total handicap. The cap is there to keep the event open to many countries. It
also means that the highest-rated players in the world cannot enter. The
federation puts its effort into that championship and into growing the game.
It does not campaign seriously for a return to the Olympics.

## The rest of the map

Uruguay, Chile, Brazil and Mexico have real domestic polo. The game is played
in India and Pakistan, where the modern rules began, and in Spain, France,
Italy and Germany. The Gulf states have invested heavily. It is also played
in South Africa, Australia, New Zealand, Nigeria and Kenya.

<Photos ids="polo-jodhpur-1925 polo-kaduna" />

Everywhere the limit is horses. A club needs land, stables, grooms and a
supply of trained ponies. Those needs decide where polo can exist, and who
in a country can play it. The game is international, and almost everywhere
it is played by a small and wealthy group. Many of its professionals,
most of them Argentine, started as grooms.
