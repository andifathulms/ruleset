# What is contested now

Recorded as unsettled.

**Whether speed belongs.** It shares a wall with boulder and lead and nothing
else: different athletes, different training, different physics, and — because
its route is standardised — a genuine world record the other two cannot have.
Whether it is the same sport is argued at every format review, and separating
it in 2024 answered the medal question without answering that one.

**Route-setting.** The largest single influence on any boulder or lead result
is a set of choices made by people who are not officials and whose work is not
reviewable. A set that favours a body type shapes a competition, and the sport
has no mechanism for challenging one.

**The point scale.** Where points sit on a route determines what the route
rewards, and those decisions are made in advance by the federation. It is the
same structure as gymnastics' difficulty values and taekwondo's technique
prices, with the same objection: a consequential rule applied by document
rather than by official.

**Format stability.** Two Olympic formats in two Games, and no guarantee of a
third. Athletes cannot plan a specialisation against a competition structure
that has not held still, and the federation's quota position means it may not
be able to promise one.

**The outdoor sport.** Competitive climbing has detached almost entirely from
the activity it came from, and a substantial part of the climbing public does
not regard the indoor competitive version as the real thing.
