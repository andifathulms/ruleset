# What is still argued about

**Does speed belong?** Speed shares a wall with boulder and lead and little
else. The athletes are different and so is the training. Its standard route
gives it a true world record, which the other two cannot have. Whether it is
the same sport is argued at every review of the format. Giving it its own
medal in 2024 settled the medal question only.

**Route-setting.** The biggest single influence on a boulder or lead result
is a set of choices made by people who are not officials. A route that
favours one body type shapes the competition. A judging decision can be
reviewed, and a route cannot.

**The point scale.** Where the points sit on a route decides what the route
rewards, and the federation decides that in advance. The objection is that an
important rule is applied by a document, with no official making the call.

**A format that will not hold still.** There have been two Olympic formats in
two Games. A climber cannot plan what to specialise in while the structure of
the competition keeps changing. The federation depends on the number of
medals it is given, so it may be unable to promise stability.

**The outdoor sport.** Competition climbing has moved a long way from the
activity it came from. Outdoor climbing has no rules, no officials and no
rankings, and a large part of the climbing public still takes its idea of
climbing from the rock.
