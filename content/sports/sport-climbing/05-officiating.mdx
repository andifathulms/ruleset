# The clock and the judge

Speed is measured by machine. There are timing pads at the top of the wall
and <Term id="false-start">false-start</Term> detection at the bottom, and
times are taken to a thousandth of a second. The route is the same
everywhere, so there is nothing to interpret.

Boulder and lead are judged by people. A judge decides whether a climber had
<Term id="control">control</Term> of a hold, which hold was reached, and
whether an attempt counts. Video review exists for appeals. The question
itself is still an assessment of a body at one moment.

## Points decided in advance

The hundred-point scale used since 2024 moved an important decision to a time
before the competition. The federation and the setters attach points to
particular holds, and set the deduction for each attempt. Where the points
sit decides what the route rewards. During the event the judges only apply
the list.

<Photo id="sport-climbing-avezou-bern-2023" />

Other sports work the same way. [Gymnastics](/sports/gymnastics/) gives each
element a value. [Taekwondo](/sports/taekwondo/) sets the worth of a head
kick or a spin. Climbing puts a price on holds. In each, a judgement that
matters a great deal is made before anyone competes, and the spectator does
not see it being made.
