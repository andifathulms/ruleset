# Where it is played

Competition climbing follows commercial climbing walls, and the walls follow
city money. The map is still changing, because walls are still being built.

## Japan and the gyms

Japan is the leading nation, above all in boulder. It has a dense network of
commercial gyms and a competition culture older than the sport's place at the
Olympic Games. Slovenia, France, Austria, Great Britain, the United States
and South Korea are the others, and increasingly China.

## Slovenia

Slovenia has two million people and produces some of the best lead climbers
in the world. It has limestone that is easy to reach, a club structure and a
national programme.

## Speed has its own map

In speed the dominant nations are Indonesia, China, Poland and Ukraine. The
standard route explains it. The climb is identical everywhere, so a country
can build one wall and train against the best times in the world without
leaving home. Veddriq Leonardo of Indonesia won the Olympic speed title in
Paris in 2024.

## The wall is the barrier

Outdoor climbing has a different geography again. It follows the rock, and
the competitive and recreational sides of climbing have less and less to do
with each other.

For competition the barrier everywhere is the wall. It is a building and it
costs money, and a town either has one or does not.
[Gymnastics](/sports/gymnastics/) and [swimming](/sports/swimming/) face the
same limit on a larger scale.
