# What is still argued about

**How tight a hoop should be.** A championship hoop leaves a fraction of a
millimetre around the ball. Tighter hoops reward precision and make games
longer. Looser hoops do the opposite. Players argue about the setting all the
time.

**Whether the break is a good thing.** The <Term id="break">break</Term> is
what makes association croquet different from every other game. It also makes
the game hard to learn, hard to watch and miserable to lose to.

**Older members.** Club players are old in most of the sport's strongholds.
Golf croquet is the main way clubs find new ones, because a newcomer can play
it straight away.

**Honesty with money on it.** The laws depend on players calling faults that
only they can see. That works in a small amateur sport. Nobody knows what
would happen if there were money in it.

**The lawns.** Croquet needs kept grass and a club that owns it. When a club
closes, its players have nowhere else to go.
