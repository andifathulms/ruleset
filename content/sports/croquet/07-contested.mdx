# What is contested now

Recorded as unsettled.

**Which code is croquet.** Association croquet is the historic championship
game and golf croquet is the larger one. The federation runs both, the growth
is in the simpler code, and the traditional powers are strongest in the harder
one while Egypt dominates the other. Nobody proposes choosing, and the question
keeps answering itself.

**Whether the break is a feature.** It is what makes association croquet
distinctive and what makes it hard to watch, hard to learn and dispiriting to
lose to. The sport's entire structural apparatus — bisques, time limits, a
second code — exists because of it.

**Hoop tolerance.** How tight a championship hoop should be is argued
continually, and it directly sets how long games last and how much precision
beats tactics.

**An ageing membership.** Club croquet's participation is old in most of its
strongholds, and golf croquet is the main recruitment tool precisely because it
is immediately playable.

**Self-officiation under pressure.** The laws depend on players calling faults
only they can see. It works, and it works because the sport is small and
amateur, and nobody has tested what would happen if there were money in it.

**The lawns.** Croquet needs maintained grass and clubs that own it. Where a
club folds the sport does not relocate, because there is nowhere to relocate to.
