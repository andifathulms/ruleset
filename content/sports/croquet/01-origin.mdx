# The game that founded Wimbledon

Croquet reached England in the 1850s, probably from Ireland, and spread very
fast. Men and women could play it together, in ordinary clothes, on a garden
lawn. Very few games of the time allowed any of that. Makers sold sets by the
hundred thousand.

<Photo id="croquet-homer-1866" />

Every household had its own rules, and competition needed one set. The first
championship was held in 1867. The All England Croquet Club, the club at
Wimbledon, was founded in 1868 to run the sport properly. [The laws it
settled](#croquet-1868-codification) were built around one rule. A player
whose ball hits another ball, a <Term id="roquet">roquet</Term>, earns two
more strokes. That rule is what makes a long
<Term id="break">break</Term> possible.

## Wimbledon, 1877

Nine years after it was founded, [the club added lawn tennis to its
name](#croquet-1877-lawns-to-tennis). That year it held the first Wimbledon
championship, on its croquet lawns.

[Tennis](/sports/tennis/) was faster and more physical, it could be played on
the same grass, and the younger members preferred it. Within a few years the
club had dropped croquet from its title. Later it put the word back as a
courtesy. The most famous tennis ground in the world is a croquet club that
was taken over by the game it let in. Croquet in England lost its lawns, its
club and many of its players inside a decade.

## Paris, 1900

[Croquet was played at one Olympic Games](#croquet-1900-olympic), in Paris in
1900. The Games were run alongside the Paris Exposition and had a loose,
sprawling programme. Almost all the croquet players were French. Women were
among them, which makes croquet one of the first Olympic sports in which women
took part.

The tournament is reported to have drawn one paying spectator, an Englishman
who travelled from Nice. Croquet has not been at the Games since.

<Photo id="croquet-paris-1900" />

The World Croquet Federation was formed in 1986. It holds the world
championships and links the national associations. Neither form of the game
has a professional circuit anywhere.
