# Where it is played

A hoop and a hard surface are all basketball needs. The court fits in a
schoolyard or between two buildings, and it does not need the large flat field
that [football](/sports/football/) does. That has made basketball the main
city sport in much of the world.

<Photos ids="basketball-palawan basketball-rucker" />

It can also be practised alone. Shooting is a skill for one person, the
target does not move, and every shot shows at once whether it worked. Half a
court is enough for a game, and so are three players a side.

## The United States and everyone else

The competitive map used to be simple: the United States, then everyone else.
The international game had its own champions all the same. Lithuania won the
European title in 1937, five years after FIBA was founded.

The United States team of 1992, the first with professionals, was the high
point of that old map. It also began to end it. The audience that team created
around the world produced a generation of players from other countries, and
many of them now play in the professional league itself. The international
game has closed the gap, and in several tournaments other nations have
finished ahead of the United States.

Women's basketball has a narrower map. It is strongest where national
federations paid for the game early.
