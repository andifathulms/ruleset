# What is still argued about

**The three-point line.** The <Term id="three-point-arc">arc</Term> was added
to open up the floor, and it did. Four decades later the complaint is that it
worked too well. Teams shoot from long range because the arithmetic tells them
to, and the shot from the middle distance has largely disappeared. People have
proposed moving the line, adding a four-point zone, or changing the value of a
shot with its distance. None of these has been adopted. Any of them would
break the sport's statistics a second time.

**Rest.** Professional teams sometimes rest healthy players to manage their
workload. Leagues have answered with rules about who must be available to
play. These are rules about the calendar, and they say nothing about the game
on the court.

**The rim.** Players have grown and the rim has not. Height turns directly
into a better shooting angle and a longer reach, and no amount of training
adds it. Nobody proposes raising the rim, because every record in the sport
would stop meaning anything.
