# What is still argued about

**Pitchers' arms.** Throwing a baseball at professional speed puts forces on
the elbow close to what the ligament can bear. Surgery to rebuild the elbow is
close to a routine event in a pitcher's career, and pitching speeds keep
rising. No rule has been written about it. The sport once
[moved the mound](#mlb-1969-mound-and-strike-zone) to raise scoring, and it
has moved nothing to protect its pitchers.

**The ball.** The number of home runs keeps changing in ways the ball's
specification does not explain. The sport has never given a full account of
why. Until it does, every power-hitting record of the last decade is open to
doubt.

**The machine and the strike zone.** The technology to call balls and strikes
works. The open question is whether framing, a catcher's trained skill, should
go on existing.

**The shift rule.** Supporters say the 2023 rule restored a balance that data
had destroyed. Opponents say a sport should not punish correct thinking.

**The Olympics.** Baseball returns to the Olympic programme in 2028. Whether
its best players will be released to play is not settled.
