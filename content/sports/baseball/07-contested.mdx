# What is contested now

Recorded as unsettled.

**Automated balls and strikes.** The technology works and has been trialled for
years. The sport has not adopted it at the top level, and the argument is not
about accuracy — it is about whether pitch framing, a real and trained skill,
should continue to exist. Baseball is the only sport on this site actively
declining a working automation for a stated reason other than error rate.

**Whether the shift ban was right.** Restricting where fielders may stand
legislates against a conclusion teams reached by analysing the sport's own
data. Supporters say it restored a balance the data had destroyed; opponents
say a sport should not punish correct thinking. The same argument attaches to
basketball's illegal-defence rules and football's 1925 offside change, and it
has never been settled in any of them.

**The ball.** Home run rates keep moving in ways that the specification does
not explain, and the sport has never satisfactorily accounted for it. Until it
does, every power record of the last decade carries an asterisk nobody has
written down.

**Pitcher injuries.** Elbow reconstruction is close to a routine career event,
velocity keeps rising, and no rule has ever been written about it. This is the
largest unaddressed problem in the sport and the one this site finds most
striking: a governing body that will move the pitching mound to raise scoring
has not moved anything to stop its pitchers breaking.

**Olympic participation.** The sport returns in 2028 without a settled answer
on whether its best players will be released to play.
