# What is contested now

Recorded as unsettled, and one of these questions is whether the sport
continues.

**The social licence.** The FEI's own commission reported that public consent
for equestrian sport cannot be assumed. Nobody inside the sport disputes that
the finding is real; what is disputed is what follows from it. One position is
that welfare reform is the price of continuing and should be pursued
aggressively. Another is that no amount of reform satisfies people who object
to horses in sport in principle, and that the federation is negotiating with a
constituency that cannot be satisfied.

**Whether dressage can be defended.** The discipline attracts the most
criticism, because its training methods are the least visible and its
appearance the most stylised. Rollkur and related practices have been
restricted repeatedly and the argument has not closed.

**Teams of three.** The sport's own rule pushes a rider to continue when its
own welfare position says stop. The federation added substitutions rather than
restoring the fourth rider, and riders continue to argue that this is the wrong
fix.

**Endurance.** The discipline with the worst record, regulated separately and
repeatedly, and still producing the incidents that damage the whole sport.

**Whether the short format was right.** Twenty years on, eventing still argues
about whether removing the endurance phases made the sport safer or simply
made it a different sport that is easier to stage.
