# What is still argued about

**The social licence.** The FEI's commission reported that public consent for
horse sport cannot be assumed. People inside the sport accept the finding and
disagree about what follows. Some say welfare reform is the price of
continuing and should go further. Others say no reform will satisfy people
who object to horses in sport on principle.

**Dressage training.** <Term id="dressage">Dressage</Term> attracts the most
criticism. Its training is the least visible and its look is the most
stylised. Rollkur and related practices have been restricted several times,
and the argument has not ended.

**Teams of three.** The federation added substitutions and kept teams at
three. Riders still say this is the wrong fix.

**Endurance.** The discipline has been regulated again and again, and it
still produces incidents that damage the whole sport.

**The short format.** Twenty years on, eventers still argue about whether
removing the endurance phases made the sport safer, or only made a different
sport that is easier to stage.
