# What is still argued about

**Whether the sensors improved the sport.** They ended the judging crisis, and
almost nobody wants to go back. They also led to a decade of rule
changes: new point values, a smaller contest area, and the end of the running
total in 2022. People in the sport disagree about whether that is good
government or a sport chasing its own equipment.

**The force threshold.** A number set by the federation decides how hard a
kick must be to count, and it differs by weight category. Changing it changes
who wins. It receives very little of the attention that a change to a playing
rule would.

**Best of three.** A competitor can score more points across a contest and
lose it. Supporters say every round now matters. Opponents say a fight should
be won by the person who did more.

**The sport and the martial art.** Millions of people practise taekwondo as a
graded course of forms and technique. Olympic sparring has moved far from
that. Some within taekwondo argue that the Olympic version no longer
represents the discipline.

**Head kicks.** Making a kick to the head worth more was a scoring decision,
and it leads to more kicks to the head. The sport has
<Term id="head-protector">head protectors</Term> with sensors in them. It has
not published a settled position on the long-term risk.
