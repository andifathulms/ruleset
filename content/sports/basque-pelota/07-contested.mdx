# What is contested now

Recorded as unsettled.

**The hands.** Professional hand players accumulate permanent damage as a
condition of competing, and the sport has no equipment remedy that would not
turn mano into a different specialty. Management is the only answer anybody
has, and it is not a solution.

**Too many specialties.** A dozen combinations of court and implement, each
contested separately, in a sport with a small international base. The argument
for choosing a flagship recurs and nobody will be the one to end a tradition.

**Frontball.** Designed for export and dismissed by purists. It is also the
only form with a plausible path into countries that will never build a fronton,
and the federation knows it.

**Betting.** Live wagering called aloud during matches is part of what the
professional hand game is, and it carries the integrity exposure that comes
with it. Regulated as gambling, not as sport.

**Who runs the professional game.** Private promotion companies contract the
players and schedule the matches; the federation runs world championships in
specialties with far smaller audiences. Neither arrangement is going to change.

**Whether jai alai comes back.** American frontons have closed almost
completely and a few have persisted in reduced forms tied to gambling licences.
A sport that exists because a betting statute requires it to be staged is not
in a healthy position, and it is the clearest cautionary case on this site of
what happens when the reason for a sport's existence is not the sport.
