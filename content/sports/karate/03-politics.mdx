# Governing body politics

Karate's governance has been dominated by two things: the fragmentation of the
art itself, and a twenty-year Olympic campaign that produced one appearance.

**Fragmentation** came first. Karate is not one thing. Shotokan, Goju-ryu,
Wado-ryu, Shito-ryu and many other styles have their own organisations,
syllabuses and competition traditions, and the WKF's authority is over a
competitive format rather than over the art. A federation representing a
practice this divided has less standing with its own practitioners than most
bodies on this site.

**The Olympic campaign** ran for decades. Karate bid repeatedly and was
repeatedly rejected, and finally entered through the host-city route: Tokyo
proposed it for 2020, alongside baseball and softball, skateboarding, sport
climbing and surfing.

Then in December 2020 the Paris organisers announced their additional sports
and karate was not among them — a decision taken before the Tokyo competition
had been held. Skateboarding, climbing and surfing, added at the same time,
were retained. Karate and baseball-softball were not.

The federation's argument since has been about the mechanism rather than the
merits: host-city discretion means a sport can be admitted for one Games by one
organiser and dropped by the next, and no sport can build on an appearance it
is not allowed to repeat. Breaking made the same argument four years later.

Karate's exclusion is therefore not a judgement about karate. It is a
consequence of how additional sports are chosen, and this site records it as a
commercial cause for that reason.
