# One Olympic Games

Karate has been contested at one Olympic Games, in Tokyo in 2021, and it was
dropped from the next before that competition took place.

## The way in

Karate asked to join the Olympic programme for decades and was turned down
each time. It got in by another door. A host city may propose extra sports for
its own Games. Tokyo proposed karate, together with
[baseball](/sports/baseball/) and [softball](/sports/softball/),
[skateboarding](/sports/skateboarding/),
[sport climbing](/sports/sport-climbing/) and [surfing](/sports/surfing/).
[The proposal was accepted in August 2016](#ioc-2016-karate-added): three
<Term id="kumite">kumite</Term> weight categories and one kata event for men,
and the same for women.

<Photo id="karate-tokyo-kumite" />

## The way out

A host city's proposal lasts for one Games. The Paris organisers proposed a
different set of sports, and [by December 2020 karate was off the 2024
programme](#ioc-2020-karate-dropped). The Tokyo Games, held in 2021, were still
to come. Skateboarding, sport climbing and surfing, which came
in beside karate, were all kept. Karate, baseball and softball were not.

So the karate competitors who walked into the Nippon Budokan in 2021 already
knew there would be no second Games to train for.

## The federation's argument

The federation has argued about the method and left the merits aside. A sport
can be let in by one organiser and dropped by the next, and no sport can build
on an appearance it is not allowed to repeat.
[Breaking](/sports/breaking/) made the same argument a few years later.
