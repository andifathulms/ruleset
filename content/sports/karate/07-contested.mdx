# What is still argued about

**Host-city sports.** A sport let in by one host and dropped by the next
cannot build on its appearance. It cannot plan how to develop its athletes, or
tell them what they are training for. Breaking made the same argument in 2023,
and the method of choosing has not changed.

**Whether control can be judged the same way every time.** Video review helps
with facts and not with the main question. Every major competition produces
disputes about whether a technique had control, form and distance.

**The kata scale.** Points made kata easier to explain and to compare. The
styles still disagree about what correct technique is, and about whether
seventy per cent technical and thirty per cent athletic rewards the right
things.

**Whether to keep asking.** The federation continues to seek a return to the
Olympic programme. Nothing in the way extra sports are chosen suggests a
different result.
