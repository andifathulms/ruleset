# What is contested now

Recorded as unsettled.

**Host-city discretion.** Karate's central argument is not about karate. A
sport admitted by one host organiser and dropped by the next cannot build on
its appearance, cannot plan a development pathway around it, and cannot tell
its athletes what they are training towards. Breaking made the same argument in
2023, and the mechanism has not changed.

**Whether controlled contact can be judged consistently.** The sport's founding
rule creates a judging problem it cannot instrument its way out of. Video review
helps with facts and not with the central question, and every major competition
produces disputes about whether a technique had control, form and distance.

**Kata's scale.** Moving to points in 2019 made kata explicable and comparable.
Whether the seventy-thirty technical-to-athletic weighting rewards the right
thing — and whether an absolute scale suits a form whose styles disagree about
correctness — is argued between the traditions the WKF represents.

**Style fragmentation.** The WKF governs a competitive format, not the art.
Major styles maintain their own organisations and competitions, and a
substantial part of the karate world does not compete under WKF rules at all.
That limits what any Olympic result means about karate as a whole.

**Whether to keep campaigning.** The federation continues to seek
reinstatement. Nothing in the current selection mechanism suggests a different
outcome.
