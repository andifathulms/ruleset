# A suit, a belt and some padding

A karate competitor needs very little. There is the
<Term id="karategi">karategi</Term>, a red or a blue belt so the officials can
tell the two competitors apart, <Term id="mitts">mitts</Term>, a gum shield,
and protection for the shins and insteps. Younger competitors also wear body
protectors and face shields.

<Photo id="karate-kumite-kit" />

The padding is there for the mistake. A strike that lands hard is a penalty,
so the mitts protect against the technique that goes wrong. In
[boxing](/sports/boxing/) the gloves do the reverse job: they protect the
hands so that a boxer can hit again and again.

None of this equipment measures anything. In
[taekwondo](/sports/taekwondo/) the padding carries sensors and counts the
kicks. Karate's is still cloth and foam.
