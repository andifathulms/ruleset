# What is contested now

Recorded as unsettled. Nothing here is a rule change, because none of it has
been decided; it is on this page because a site about why rules change should
be able to show the arguments before they resolve.

**Scoring, again.** The federation has repeatedly considered replacing 21-point
rally scoring with a best-of-five format to 11 points, and the membership has
repeatedly declined. The stated case is the same one that produced the 2006
change and the abandoned 2002 experiment: shorter, more predictable matches for
broadcast. The case against is that 21-point games give a losing player time to
recover a match, and that the sport has already rewritten its scoring twice in
twenty years for the same reason without settling the question.

**Service height.** The fixed 1.15 metres solved the inconsistency of judging
against a player's own body, and introduced a different complaint: a single
absolute height is not neutral between a player of 1.60 m and one of 1.95 m
either, it just moves who is disadvantaged. Whether the rule should scale with
the player is an open argument, and it is the same argument the sport had before
2018 running in the opposite direction.

**Shuttle supply.** Feathered shuttles depend on goose and duck feathers of a
specific grade, and cost and availability have both moved. Synthetic shuttles
approved for general play behave measurably differently in flight. Whether top
competition should move to synthetics is a question about cost and sustainability
that would, if answered yes, be an equipment change of exactly the kind that has
severed number series in other sports on this site.
