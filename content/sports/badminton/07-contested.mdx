# What is still argued about

None of this has been decided, so none of it is a rule yet.

**Shorter games.** The federation has repeatedly proposed replacing games to
21 with best of five games to 11, and its members have repeatedly said no. The
argument for is shorter, more predictable matches for broadcast, the same
argument that produced the 2006 change. The argument against is that a game to
21 gives a player who starts badly time to recover.

**The service height.** A fixed 1.15 metres ended the arguments about judging,
and started another. One height is not neutral between a player of 1.60 m and
a player of 1.95 m: the taller player must serve from a lower point relative
to their body. Whether the limit should scale with the player is an open
argument, and it is the old rule coming back from the other direction.

**Feathers.** Feathered <Term id="shuttle">shuttles</Term> depend on goose and
duck feathers of a particular grade, and both the cost and the supply have
moved. Synthetic shuttles fly measurably differently. Whether top competition
should switch is a question about cost and sustainability, and a yes would
change how the game is played at the highest level.
