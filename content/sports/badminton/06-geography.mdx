# Where it is played

Badminton was codified in England and is played, watched and won mostly in
Asia.

<Photo id="badminton-istora-2026" />

China, Indonesia, Malaysia, South Korea, Japan, Denmark and Chinese Taipei win
very nearly all of the medals. Denmark is the one European nation that has
stayed at the top for decades, on the strength of a deep club system. That
suggests the pattern comes from where people have somewhere to play, not from
anything innate.

<Photos ids="badminton-lin-dan-2012 badminton-axelsen-2018" />

The administration took a long time to follow the players. The international
federation was run from England for seventy years and moved to Kuala Lumpur in
2005.

For the strongest nations the hardest tournament can be the national trial.
Where a country has more top players than Olympic places, players who would
win medals for another nation do not qualify at all.

Outside competition it is one of the easiest games to start. Across South and
Southeast Asia it is played on beaches and in streets with no court, because
two rackets and a shuttle are enough for a game.
