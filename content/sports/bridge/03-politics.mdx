# The court that said it is not a sport

Bridge has a world federation, a championship cycle and recognition from the
IOC. In 2017 a European court ruled that it is not a sport.

The case began with a tax exemption. The English Bridge Union asked to be
treated as a sport, so that the exemption would apply. The question went up
to the court: what does "sport" mean in that law? The court's answer was that
duplicate bridge does not qualify, because it does not involve a significant
physical element. The ruling [changed no rule of the game](#bridge-2017-not-a-sport),
but it had money attached.

<Photo id="bridge-playing-area-2014" />

The federation's reply was that the IOC and many national bodies recognise
bridge as a sport, and that a tax definition is not a general verdict. That
reply did not change the ruling. [Cheerleading](/sports/cheerleading/) has
faced the same question in American courts.

Elsewhere bridge has been treated as a sport. It was played at the Asian Games
in 2018, when Indonesia was the host. It has never been on an Olympic
programme.
