# What is still argued about

None of this is settled.

**Catching online cheats.** The evidence is statistical. Accusations can end
a career, and the high-profile cases have divided the game.

**The innocent partner.** Most agree that the rule on
<Term id="unauthorised-information">unauthorised information</Term> is right
in principle. It still produces rulings that feel unjust to the player who
loses them, and every serious event has disputes about it.

**Complex systems.** Some players see highly artificial bidding systems as the
game's intellectual frontier. Others see them as a barrier to newcomers. The
limits on them are argued over between the two.

**The Asian Games.** Bridge was played in 2018. Whether it returns depends on
hosts willing to stage a sport that needs quiet rooms and offers little for a
crowd to watch.

**Is it a sport?** A court said no in 2017, for tax purposes. The IOC's
recognition and the Asian Games say yes. Funding in several countries depends
on the answer.
