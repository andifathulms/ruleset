# What is still argued about

**Two winners.** Sweden and Finland have taken almost every title. The
federation's divisions and development programmes have not changed that. A
sport that wants an Olympic place has to show it is played well around the
world, and floorball cannot yet show it.

**The Olympic campaign.** The federation has had Olympic recognition since
2011 and a World Games place since 2017. Handball, basketball and volleyball
already hold the indoor team places on the Olympic programme. Inside the
federation the question is whether to keep campaigning or to settle as a
school and club sport.

**The blade.** The curve of the <Term id="blade">blade</Term> is capped and
measured, and makers keep working at the edge of the limit. A 23-gram ball
reacts strongly to small changes in a blade.

**The name "indoor hockey".** Floorball is often described as a kind of
hockey. It has no offside, no checking, a stick kept below the waist and a
goalkeeper with no stick, and those add up to a different game. The label
affects how broadcasters present the sport and which national bodies will
fund it.
