# What is contested now

Recorded as unsettled.

**Whether two countries winning everything can be fixed.** Sweden and Finland
have taken almost every title the sport has offered. World championship
division structures and development programmes are the federation's answers,
and none has changed the outcome. Until it does, the universality argument
required for Olympic inclusion is not available.

**Olympic candidacy.** IOC recognition since 2011, a World Games place since
2017, and no realistic route onto a programme where handball, basketball and
volleyball already occupy the indoor team-sport slots. Whether to keep
campaigning, or to consolidate as a school and club sport, is a genuine
strategic question inside the federation.

**Stick height enforcement.** The rule that keeps the sport safe is also the
one that penalises a natural follow-through, and how strictly it should be
called is argued at every level.

**The blade curve.** Capped and measured, and manufacturers keep pressing
against the limit. A sport with a 23-gram ball is unusually sensitive to what
a blade can do to it.

**Whether it should stop being called indoor hockey.** Floorball is routinely
described as a version of a sport it is not: no offside, no checking, a
waist-height cap and a goalkeeper without a stick add up to a different game.
The federation's naming and positioning problem is real, and it affects
everything from broadcast framing to which national sports bodies will fund it.
