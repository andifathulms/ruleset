# Where they are played

Each of the three games has its own map.

## Chess

Chess is played seriously almost everywhere. Its historic strength is Russian
and post-Soviet. Russia, Armenia, Ukraine, Georgia, Azerbaijan and Uzbekistan
all stand far above their size. The United States, China, India, Iran and
much of Europe have deep structures too. India is rising fastest. China has
been a leading nation in both the men's and the women's game for two decades.

<Photos ids="board-uzbekistan-2022 board-karpov-1977" />

In the last twenty years chess has stopped depending on place. On an online
platform, a player anywhere with a connection can play rated games against
opponents of the same strength at any hour. Much of the world's competitive
chess is now played by people who have never entered a tournament hall. Go
and xiangqi have not moved online to nearly the same extent.

## Go

Professional go is East Asian. China, Japan and South Korea have full
professional systems that take children full-time from a young age, and
those systems produce the best players in the world. Everywhere else the game
is amateur.

## Xiangqi

Xiangqi is Chinese, and it is mostly played outdoors and in public. It
follows the Chinese diaspora. It is deeply established in Vietnam and in
Chinese communities across Southeast Asia and beyond.
