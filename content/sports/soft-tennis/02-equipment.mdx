# The ball and the racket

Everything in soft tennis starts from the ball.

The <Term id="soft-ball">ball</Term> is hollow rubber filled with air. It
weighs about 30 grams and is about 6.6 centimetres across, with no felt cover.
A tennis ball is about twice as heavy. It is pressurised, and its felt grips
the strings and the court.

The soft ball slows down fast, so the court feels bigger and players reach
shots that would beat them in tennis. It squashes on the strings and stays
there longer, so spin works differently. A good opponent cannot be beaten by
a flat, hard drive. A point has to be built, shot by shot.

<Photo id="soft-tennis-korea-1923" />

The racket followed the ball. It is much lighter than a tennis racket and
strung much more loosely. A tight string bed cannot control a soft ball, and a
heavy frame is not needed when the ball carries so little pace. Modern frames
are graphite and composite, like tennis frames. They started from the same
place and went in a different direction.

<Photo id="soft-tennis-racket-ball" />

That is why the split never closed. A soft tennis racket is no use for
[tennis](/sports/tennis/), and a tennis racket is no use for soft tennis. There
was never a moment when the two games could quietly join again.

Other sports have changed a material to save money or to get a better object.
[Sepak takraw](/sports/sepak-takraw/) moved from rattan balls to synthetic
ones, [boules](/sports/boules/) from wood to steel, and
[bowling](/sports/bowling/) from urethane to resin. Each new object did the old
one's job. In soft tennis, the cheaper ball made a second sport, and both
sports are still played.
