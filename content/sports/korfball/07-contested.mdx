# What is still argued about

**The defended shot.** The rule holds the whole design together, and it is
the rule most often blamed when a match goes static. Nobody inside the sport
proposes removing it. The argument is about how strictly to apply it.

**Short formats.** Beach korfball is played four a side on sand. Korfball4 is
a shorter four-a-side game written for smaller venues and for broadcast. Both
exist to make the game easier to stage and to follow for people who have never
seen it. Whether so small a federation should keep up several versions of the
game, or put everything into one, is an open question.

**The Olympic campaign.** The Netherlands and Belgium have won almost
everything. That record works against any claim that the game is played
everywhere, and nobody has a clear way to change it. Whether to go on
campaigning at all is argued too.
