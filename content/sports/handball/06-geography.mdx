# Where it is played

Handball is a European game. Denmark, France, Germany, Norway, Sweden, Spain,
Croatia, Hungary, Slovenia, Poland and Montenegro are the leading nations.
Outside Europe the strongest are Egypt, Qatar, Brazil and South Korea.

<Photos ids="handball-egypt-2011 handball-qatar-2015" />

Almost all the professional game is in Europe too. Much of it is in Germany
and France, whose leagues employ most of the world's best players. The big
club competitions are European. The federation wants the game to grow around
the world, and the money that pays for it stays in one region.

The cause is history. Handball was invented in northern Europe and taught in
European schools. It did not travel with empire or emigration, as
[football](/sports/football/), [cricket](/sports/cricket/) and
[rugby](/sports/rugby/) did. It grew where it was taught and almost nowhere
else.

The building matters as well. A handball court is larger than a
[basketball](/sports/basketball/) or [volleyball](/sports/volleyball/) court,
and it needs an indoor hall. In most of the world those two sports already
fill the halls, and handball usually loses the contest for space.

## The beach

Beach handball is the federation's answer. It is played outdoors on sand by
four a side and is cheaper to stage, and a spectacular goal counts double. It
has not yet changed the map.

## The women's game

The women's map is similar. Norway, France, Denmark and the Netherlands are
the strongest nations, and the professional side of the women's game is about
as developed as the men's.
