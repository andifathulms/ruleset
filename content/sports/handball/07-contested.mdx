# What is still argued about

**The empty net.** A decade on, handball has not decided whether it likes
attacking with seven. Supporters say it is exciting and rewards risk.
Opponents say a game with a goalkeeper should not routinely be played without
one. They add that the punishment, a long throw into an
<Term id="empty-net">empty net</Term>, fails too often to stop teams trying.
People have proposed making the change of goalkeeper take longer, or making a
goal into an empty net count for more. Neither idea has been adopted.

**Resin.** Everybody at the top uses it, it changes how well players handle
the ball, and many halls will not host handball because of it. The rules still
say nothing.

**Contact.** Handball allows more contact than other indoor court games, and
shoulder and knee injuries are common. The argument about where blocking ends
and dangerous play begins comes back after every major tournament.
