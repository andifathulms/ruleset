# What is contested now

Recorded as unsettled.

**The empty net.** A decade after the rule that permitted it, the sport has not
settled whether it likes what it made. Supporters say the seven-against-six
attack is exciting and rewards risk. Opponents say a sport with a goalkeeper
position should not be routinely played without one, and that the tactic
survives because the punishment — a long shot into an open net — is less
reliable than it should be.

Proposals to restrict it, by requiring a delay or by making the empty-net goal
worth more, circulate and have not been adopted.

**Resin.** Universal, performance-relevant, unregulated, and the reason a
significant number of halls will not host handball. The sport has never
legislated on a substance that shapes both how it is played and where.

**Contact.** Handball permits more contact than any other indoor court sport
here, and its shoulder and knee injury rates reflect that. Whether the balance
between defensive obstruction and dangerous play sits in the right place is
argued after every major tournament.

**The map.** The IHF's development ambitions are global and its professional
economy is European. Beach handball is the intended answer and has not yet
delivered one, and the sport competes for indoor facilities with basketball and
volleyball almost everywhere outside Europe.
