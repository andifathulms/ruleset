# What is still argued about

**The surface.** Fifty years on, the cost of a proper pitch is still hockey's
biggest problem. Turf that needs little or no water is being approved. The
argument is whether it plays well enough for top matches, or will only create
a second kind of pitch for a second tier of nations.

**The penalty corner.** A large share of international goals come from the
<Term id="penalty-corner">penalty corner</Term>. Its defenders run at a drag
flick struck from about fifteen metres. It is the sport's signature and its
main injury risk. Proposals to change the number of defenders, the
<Term id="injection">injection</Term> or the penalty itself come up again and
again.

<Photo id="hockey-corner-defence" />

**Removing more rules.** Offside went, and it worked. Obstruction is the usual
next candidate. It is the least consistent call in the game and the only one
that asks the umpire about purpose. It has survived every review so far.

**Hockey5s.** The five-a-side game has its own rules. People disagree about
whether it is a way into hockey or a rival to it. [Rugby](/sports/rugby/) and
[lacrosse](/sports/lacrosse/) both reached the Olympic programme with shorter
formats, and the federation has noticed.
