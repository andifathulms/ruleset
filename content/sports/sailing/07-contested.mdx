# What is still argued about

**The medal race.** Sailors have called it a lottery since 2008. The
federation answers that a regatta cannot be broadcast if its winner is known
before the last race. Neither side has moved.

**Changing the classes.** One side says a sport cannot ask athletes to spend
ten years on a boat that it reviews every four. The other side says a fixed
programme drifts away from the way the sport is really sailed, as it did
before foiling was let in.

**Cost.** The federation says it wants more people and more countries
sailing. Many of its equipment choices are expensive. The kite and
windsurfing classes pull one way and the foiling boats pull the other.

**Umpiring.** Umpires on the water solved the problem of timing, because
results are now final when the racing ends. They did not solve the problem of
judgement. Tracking data helps with right-of-way calls and does not settle
them.

**The venue.** Sailing is often staged far from the host city, and that keeps
its place on the Olympic programme under question.
