# What is still argued about

**Private ownership.** No federation controls the rules of any esport. Nobody
has proposed an arrangement that would change this without a publisher giving
up its property.

**Two federations.** Which of the two speaks for esports is disputed between
them. It matters little to the publishers.

**Player welfare.** Professionals start very young and practise for very long
hours. Peak ages are in the low twenties, and many retire in their
mid-twenties. Repetitive strain injuries and unfair contracts have led the
larger leagues to set minimum ages, contract standards and required rest. The
leagues made those reforms. The governing bodies did not.

**When a game dies.** A discipline can end because its owner stops supporting
it, and the careers of its professionals end with it. A world champion can
lose the game while still at their peak.
