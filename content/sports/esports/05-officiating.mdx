# Officiating

The cleanest adjudication of play on this site, and the messiest adjudication
of everything else.

**Play needs no officials at all.** Whether a shot connected, whether an
ability landed, whether a player was inside a zone are not judgements — the
server already calculated them, deterministically, and the result is not
appealable. No sport covered here has less to argue about regarding what
happened.

Everything an esports referee does is therefore about conditions:

**Technical pauses.** A competitor may stop play for a hardware failure,
software fault or disconnection, and the match resumes from the paused state or,
in a serious case, the game is remade from the start. This is necessary because
the field of play can fail, and it is open to abuse as a tactical reset, so its
use is regulated and logged.

**Which version.** Officials rule on the build a competition is played on and
whether a patch may land inside an event. In every other sport the laws are
fixed for the season; here somebody has to decide when to stop accepting
updates.

**Ghosting and outside information.** Booths and noise-cancelling headsets
exist because a crowd reacting at the right moment tells a team where the
opposition is. Officials police the physical environment for the same reason
bridge built screens.

**Anti-cheat and integrity.** Software monitoring on the machines, plus
statistical review of inputs, plus betting-market monitoring for fixing.

So the referee never rules on the game, and rules on almost everything around
it.
