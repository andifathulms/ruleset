# What is contested now

Recorded as unsettled.

**Whether the sport was fairly removed.** Softball's federation maintains that
the 2005 vote applied baseball's problems to a sport that did not have them.
The IOC has never separated the two cases, and since neither sport has been
restored to the permanent programme, the question stays open by default.

**The shared listing.** Being counted with baseball secured a return in 2021
and 2028. It also means softball's athletes hold no listing of their own, that
its quota is negotiated alongside a game they do not play, and that its
presence tracks whether a host city cares about baseball.

**Pitching distance.** Forty-three feet is a compromise between the pitcher's
dominance and the batter's chance, and it is argued in both directions —
whether it went far enough, and whether moving it at all was a concession the
sport did not need to make.

**Bat certification.** Regulating a bat by measured performance rather than by
dimension means the standard can change under a bat already sold, and a bat
legal in one jurisdiction may not be legal in another. Players and
manufacturers dispute both the thresholds and the testing.

**Whether men's fastpitch should exist Olympically.** The Olympic softball
event is women's only, and the Olympic baseball event is men's only. The
federation presents this as balance. It also means a men's fastpitch player and
a women's baseball player have no pathway at all.
