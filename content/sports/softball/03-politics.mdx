# Voted out with baseball

Softball lost its place at the Olympic Games in a vote that was mostly about
another sport.

The International Softball Federation was formed in 1952. Softball joined the
Olympic programme at Atlanta in 1996, as a women's event, and was played at
four Games. In 2005 the International Olympic Committee voted
[baseball](/sports/baseball/) and softball
[off the programme after 2008](#softball-2008-olympic-removal).

<Photo id="softball-beijing-2008" />

## The case against it

The two sports were treated as a pair. The objections raised were a
professional league that would not release its players, and a doping record.
Both belonged to baseball. Softball had no league holding back its athletes
and no similar case against it. It was removed anyway, and the reasons were
never separated. Softball's federation has argued ever since that the vote
was not about softball. The Olympic committee has never dealt with the two
cases apart.

## Joining the sport it was removed with

The answer was to join baseball formally. In 2013
[the two federations merged](#softball-2013-federation-merger) into the World
Baseball Softball Confederation. They campaigned together, and argued that
one listing with a men's tournament and a women's tournament was a better
offer than two sports competing for one place.

It has worked twice. Baseball and softball returned at Tokyo in 2021, as
sports added by the host city. They were absent from Paris, and they return
at Los Angeles in 2028.

## What the shared listing costs

Softball players have no Olympic listing of their own. The number of places
is negotiated alongside a game they do not play. Whether softball appears at
all depends on whether the host city cares about baseball.

The Olympic softball event is for women only, and the Olympic baseball event
is for men only. The federation presents this as balance. It also means that
a man who plays fastpitch and a woman who plays baseball have no Olympic path
at all.
