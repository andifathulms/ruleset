# A floor and a speaker

Breaking has no equipment. Dancers wear what they like. A competition
controls only two things: the floor, which must be flat and smooth, and the
sound system. The dance grew up in public spaces because it needed nothing
else.

<Photo id="breaking-hamburg-street" />

[Gymnastics](/sports/gymnastics/) describes its apparatus in great detail,
because the apparatus decides which movements are possible and so what a
difficulty value means. Breaking has no table of difficulty values. Its
judges say which of two dancers was better and never say what a performance
was worth, so there is nothing that a standard apparatus would keep equal.

The one thing that works like equipment is the music, and it is left
uncontrolled on purpose. The <Term id="dj">DJ</Term> picks tracks the dancers
have not heard. In other judged sports the conditions are fixed so that a
performance can be planned. In breaking the main condition is a surprise.
