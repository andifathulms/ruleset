# What is contested now

Recorded as unsettled.

**Whether the Olympic project was worth it.** Breaking gave up a great deal to
be admitted: an external governing body, a codified judging system, national
federations built to satisfy a selection process, and a competitive form
shaped for a broadcast. It was contested once. The community that objected in
2017 has an obvious case to make now, and the people who argued that exposure
would grow the form have their own — participation did rise.

**Whether Trivium survives.** The judging system was built for Olympic
competition and is genuinely well designed. Whether a form that no longer needs
it keeps using it, or reverts to holistic practitioner judging, is an open
question about which parts of codification stick when the reason for them goes
away.

**Who governs breaking.** The WDSF's mandate came from Olympic recognition. It
is not obvious what that mandate is now, or whether the sport wants one at all.

**The 2032 question.** Breaking could return; skateboarding, climbing and
surfing were all added and retained. What this site notes is that the 2028
exclusion was decided before the 2024 competition happened, which means the
sport has never had the chance to be judged on its performance — and that is
the fact its community is most likely to remember.
