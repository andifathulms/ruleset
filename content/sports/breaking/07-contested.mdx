# What is still argued about

**Whether the Olympic Games were worth it.** To be admitted, breaking
accepted an outside governing body, a written judging system, national
federations built for a selection process, and a form of
<Term id="battle">battle</Term> shaped for television. It was contested once.
Those who objected in 2017 can point to that. Those who said the attention
would help the dance grow can point to a rise in participation.

**Whether Trivium lasts.** The judging system was built for Olympic
competition. Breaking may keep it now that the reason is gone, or go back to
dancers judging the whole performance.

**Who governs breaking.** The World DanceSport Federation's authority came
from its Olympic recognition. It is not clear what that authority means now,
or whether breakers want a governing body at all.

**A return.** Breaking could come back to a later Games.
[Skateboarding](/sports/skateboarding/),
[sport climbing](/sports/sport-climbing/) and [surfing](/sports/surfing/)
were all added and then kept. Breaking was left out of 2028 before its 2024
competition was held, so it was never judged on how that competition went.
