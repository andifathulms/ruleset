# Where it is played

Competitive ju-jitsu under the international federation is strongest in
Europe. It was put together there, in Germany, the Netherlands, Belgium,
Austria, France, Italy, Sweden, Russia and Ukraine. The federation grew out of
the European federations, and its world championships still show it.

## Abu Dhabi

The United Arab Emirates is the exception. Abu Dhabi has spent heavily on
grappling. It hosts major events, funds programmes and has put jiu-jitsu into
the school curriculum. The UAE is now one of the powers of the sport.

## Asia

Ju-jitsu appeared at the Asian Games in 2018. Kazakhstan, Mongolia, Vietnam,
Uzbekistan and Iran all compete.

## The larger crowd

The federation's map leaves out most of the people who train. Far more people
practise grappling under the Brazilian codes, in Brazil, the United States and
across Europe, than compete under the federation. Most of them would say they
do jiu-jitsu.
