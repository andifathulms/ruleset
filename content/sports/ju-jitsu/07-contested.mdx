# What is still argued about

**Whether the three phases work.** Making a bout pass through striking,
throwing and groundwork keeps ju-jitsu different from other combat sports. It
also asks each athlete to face specialists at every range. Some inside the
federation say this gives good competition. Others say it gives a shallow
version of three better sports.

**The place of Duo.** Duo is a cooperative, judged event at the world
championship of a combat sport. It lets a large part of the art be contested.
It is also the hardest part of the sport to explain to an outsider.

**Gi and <Term id="no-gi">no-gi</Term>.** Contesting both means keeping two
technical codes and two sets of specialists under one federation. Which of the
two is the real Ne-waza is argued here just as it is in the Brazilian sport.
