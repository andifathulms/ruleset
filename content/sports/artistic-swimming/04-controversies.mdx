# Controversies that forced rule changes

**The judging.** Until 2023 difficulty was itself one of the judges'
marks, and a mark given on impression is hard to check. The scoring system
of 2023 was built to make it checkable: every element's difficulty is
declared on a coach card and verified by controllers with video review, and
the federation's stated aim was "objective and detailed measurement". It is
the move diving made with its difficulty tables.

**The name.** The rename of 2017 was decided by a congress of delegates from
every aquatic sport, and its critics within the sport said it reduced their
event to a show. FINA said it would not revert.

**The men.** Men could swim at world championships from 2015 and could not
at the Games until 2024. The change was made, but in its narrowest form:
two places in a team of eight, and no Olympic men's or mixed event.

**Russia.** Russia won every Olympic gold from 2000 to 2020, twelve in all,
the last two under the name of the Russian Olympic Committee. It was absent
from Paris 2024, and China won both events.
