# Governing body politics

The sport is governed with swimming by World Aquatics, formerly FINA, and
the two largest decisions about it in the last decade were taken by bodies
above its own technical committee.

The name came from the general congress. In July 2017 FINA's congress, in
which every member federation votes regardless of whether it has a single
synchronised swimmer, renamed the sport artistic swimming. FINA's stated
reason was to align it with artistic gymnastics and widen its appeal; its
executive director said the idea had also come from the IOC. Swimmers
protested, and a petition gathered nearly 11,000 signatures, but the vote
stood.

The men came from the IOC. On 22 December 2022 it approved men in the
Olympic team, up to two in eight, after a campaign led by athletes such as
Bill May, who had won the first mixed duet world title in 2015. No man was
selected for Paris in 2024.
