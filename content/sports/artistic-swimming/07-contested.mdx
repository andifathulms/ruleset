# What is contested now

**The base mark.** Under the 2023 system an element that does not match its
declaration gets its minimum value. In an open letter in 2024 a world
championship athlete called the system "disrespectful to the athletes'
work", arguing that a single missed movement in a hybrid of twenty is
punished as heavily as missing all of them.

**Execution against difficulty.** The same letter argued that judges use too
little of the scale — marks "mostly float between 6.75 and 8.5" — so that
declared difficulty decides results. Whether the reform has made the sport
more objective or only more predictable is the argument now.

**Men at the Games.** Two places in a team of eight, and no man selected in
2024. Whether a men's or mixed Olympic event follows is open.

**The name.** It has stuck. Whether it has changed how the sport is seen is
harder to say.
