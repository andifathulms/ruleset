# Ten judges and six controllers

A routine is marked by two panels of five judges and checked by six
<Term id="controller">technical controllers</Term>, all seated around the
pool.

The <Term id="elements-panel">Elements panel</Term> marks how well each
element was performed. The
<Term id="artistic-impression">Artistic Impression</Term> panel gives three
marks each: for choreography and musicality, for performance, and for
transitions. Every mark is from 0 to 10, in quarter points.

<Photos ids="artistic-swimming-officials-2013 artistic-swimming-igla-judges" />

Difficulty is not judged. Three difficulty controllers follow the
<Term id="coach-card">coach card</Term> and check each element as it happens.
An element that does not match its declaration gets a
<Term id="base-mark">base mark</Term>. Three synchronisation controllers count
the moments when the swimmers are not together, at 0.1, 0.5 or 3.0 points
each.

A controller does not act alone. Two of the three must agree before a base
mark or a major error is applied, and each controller can ask for one video
review per element.

Officials are also appointed to watch the bottom of the pool. A swimmer who
uses it to push off costs the team eight points.
