# Officiating

A routine is marked by two panels of five judges and checked by six
technical controllers, seated around the pool.

The Elements panel marks the execution of every element. The Artistic
Impression panel gives three marks each: for choreography and musicality,
for performance, and for transitions. Every mark is from 0 to 10 in quarter
points.

Difficulty is not judged. Each team submits a coach card listing its
elements and their declared difficulty, and three difficulty controllers
verify each one as it happens. An element that does not match its
declaration gets a base mark. Three synchronisation controllers count the
unequal actions, at 0.1, 0.5 or 3.0 points each. Two of the three must agree
to apply a base mark or a major error, and each can ask for one video
review per element.

<Photo id="artistic-swimming-brazil-2016" />

Officials are also appointed to watch the bottom of the pool. Using it to
push off costs eight points.
