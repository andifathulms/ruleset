# Where it is played

The sport began in North America and Europe, and the United States and
Canada won most of the early Olympic titles.

From 2000 it belonged to Russia. Its swimmers won every Olympic gold for
twenty years, and Svetlana Romashina retired with seven, more than anyone
else in the sport.

China, Japan, Spain and Ukraine have been the nearest challengers. China
was second to Russia for much of the 2010s and won both Olympic events in
Paris in 2024, in Russia's absence. Ukraine's team won bronze in Tokyo.

Men's artistic swimming is small and growing. Italy's Giorgio Minisini has
won world titles in the mixed duet, and men's and mixed events are now on
the world championship programme, though not on the Olympic one.
