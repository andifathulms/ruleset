# Twenty years of Russian gold

Artistic swimming began in North America and Europe, and the United States
and Canada won most of the early Olympic titles.

From 2000 it belonged to Russia. Russian swimmers won every Olympic gold from
2000 to 2020, twelve in all. The last two were won under the name of the
Russian Olympic Committee. Svetlana Romashina retired with seven gold medals,
more than anyone else in the sport.

China, Japan, Spain and Ukraine have been the nearest challengers. China was
second to Russia for much of the 2010s. Ukraine's team won bronze in Tokyo.
Russia was absent from Paris in 2024, and China won both events.

Men's artistic swimming is small and growing. Italy's Giorgio Minisini has
won world titles in the mixed duet. Men's solos and the mixed duet are on the
world championship programme and not on the Olympic one.
