# What is contested now

Recorded as unsettled.

**The discarded qualification score.** More than a decade on, shooters still
argue that resetting finals to zero produces the wrong champion — that sixty
shots of qualification is a better measure of shooting than a short
elimination final, and that a sport whose entire claim is precision has made
its most precise data irrelevant to the result.

The federation's position is that a final decided before it starts is not a
final. Both are true. Archery, sailing and taekwondo have all made the same
trade, and this site records shooting as the sharpest version because the
contradiction with the sport's own values is the most direct.

**Whether decimals go further.** Elite scores are converging again. Extending
resolution has worked twice and there is no obvious reason it would not work a
third time, but each extension changes the unit and severs the record again.

**The programme.** The 2018 restructure removed events contested for a century
to meet parity within a quota, and the sport has more disciplines than medals.
Which events return, if any, is a live argument.

**Firearms law.** Shooting's participation base and its public standing both
depend on regulation that has nothing to do with sport, and the sport has no
mechanism for influencing it. Its move towards indoor air events is the only
adaptation available.
