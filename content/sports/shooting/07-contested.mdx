# What is still argued about

**The score that is thrown away.** More than ten years on, shooters still
argue that a final from zero can produce the wrong champion. Sixty shots of
qualification, they say, measure shooting better than a short final. The
federation holds that a final decided before it starts is not a final.

**More decimals.** The scores of the best shooters are drawing close together
again. A finer scale has solved that twice. Each time it also changed the unit
and cut new records off from old ones.

**The programme.** The 2018 change removed events that had been contested for
a century, and shooting has more disciplines than it has medals. Which events
come back, if any, is an open argument.

**Firearms law.** The number of people who can take up shooting, and how the
public sees it, both depend on laws that have nothing to do with sport. The
sport has no means of changing them.
