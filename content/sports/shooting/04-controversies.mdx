# The search for an ending

For most of its history a shooting competition had nothing to watch. Shooters
fired their <Term id="series">series</Term> over several hours, each in their
own time, and a result went up on a board. It was a sound way to find the best
shooter. It gave a spectator nothing to follow.

<Photo id="shooting-bisley-1978" />

Three changes in about thirty years were made to fix that.

## 1988: a final

[A final round was added for the leading
shooters](#issf-1988-finals-added), held after
<Term id="qualification">qualification</Term>. The qualification score was
carried forward and the shots of the final were added to it. In the final
each shot was scored to a tenth of a ring, because whole rings could not
separate the best. Shooting now had a last act.

<Photo id="shooting-beijing-finals-hall" />

## 2013: start again from zero

The carried score caused its own problem. A shooter with a large lead from
qualification had often won before the final began.

[From 2013 the final starts from zero](#issf-2013-finals-from-zero).
Qualification decides only who gets into the
<Term id="final">final</Term>. Shooters then drop out one at a time, and the
last shots are fired by two people for the gold medal.

Shooters objected, and many still do. A competitor can beat the whole field
over sixty shots of qualification and finish eighth. Their argument is that
shooting claims to be a sport of precision, and its longest and most precise
measurement now has the least effect on the medal. The federation has not
disputed that description. Its answer is that a final decided before it
starts is no final at all. [Archery](/sports/archery/) made a similar trade in
2011 and [sailing](/sports/sailing/) in 2008.

<Photo id="shooting-final-2024" />

## 2013: a finer scale

By the 2010s the best air rifle shooters were posting nearly the same
whole-ring totals in qualification. [Decimal scoring was extended from the
final to qualification](#issf-2013-decimal-qualification). The scores could
separate shooters again. Every qualification record in the event was now
written in a different unit.

## 2018: events swapped for balance

The International Olympic Committee required equal numbers of events for men
and women inside a fixed number of athletes. [Three men's events were removed
and three mixed-team events were
added](#issf-2018-programme-restructure). The men's 50 metre free pistol and
50 metre rifle prone, both contested for decades, were among those that went.
