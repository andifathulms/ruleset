# A singlet, shoes and a scale

A wrestler wears a singlet and a pair of shoes, and in some competitions
headgear. There is no implement and no protective kit that matters, so there
has been almost nothing for the rules to argue about.

<Photo id="wrestling-byers-lopez-2009" />

The mat is the only other piece of equipment. It went from canvas over
sawdust to modern foam, and that made the sport safer without anyone having
to fight over a rule.

<Photo id="wrestling-leipzig-1955" />

## The body is the equipment

What wrestling measures is the wrestler. Every competitor belongs to a weight
category and must pass a <Term id="weigh-in">weigh-in</Term>.

That is where the sport's most serious health problem lives. Wrestlers dry
themselves out severely to make a lower category, then drink again before
they compete. The practice has been linked to deaths in college wrestling in
the United States in the 1990s. The rules there changed afterwards.

The international answer has been about procedure: when the weigh-in happens,
a weigh-in on each day in some competitions, and how much margin is allowed.
