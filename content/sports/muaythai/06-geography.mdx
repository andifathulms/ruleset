# Thailand, and everywhere else

Thailand is the centre of muaythai. The stadiums, the gyms, the fight economy
and the great majority of elite fighters are Thai. A foreign fighter who
wanted to reach the top has usually had to go there and fight under Thai
conditions.

Outside Thailand the sport is spread very widely. Russia, Ukraine, Belarus
and Kazakhstan are strong under the amateur rules. So are Britain, France,
Italy, Sweden, Turkey and Iran. Brazil, the United States, Australia and
Japan all have large scenes.

## More people train than fight

Around the world, a very large number of people train muaythai in commercial
gyms for fitness and never mean to compete. Pad work, bag work and
conditioning make a full session with no sparring. Muaythai spread as a way
of training first and as a competition second.
[Judo](/sports/judo/), [taekwondo](/sports/taekwondo/) and
[wrestling](/sports/wrestling/) travelled the other way round.

## The neighbours

Cambodia, Laos and Myanmar have closely related codes of their own: kun
khmer, muay lao and lethwei. Lethwei also allows headbutts. What the shared
tradition should be called is a live dispute in the region.
