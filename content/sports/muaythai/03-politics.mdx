# The federation and the stadiums

The international federation governs muaythai everywhere except at the top.

IFMA was formed in 1993 and built a real international structure. It has been
fully recognised by the Olympic movement since 2021 and has a place at the
World Games.

It does not govern the professional stadium system in Thailand, where the
best fighters in the world compete. <Term
id="stadium-muaythai">Stadium muaythai</Term> has different rules, different
equipment and a different way of scoring. It is a domestic industry with its
own institutions and its own money, and with legal betting at ringside. The
stadiums are not defying the federation. They are doing what the sport's
homeland has done for a century.

<Photo id="muaythai-lumpinee" />

[Sumo](/sports/sumo/) has the same structure: an internationally recognised
federation runs its own competitions, and the summit of the sport is a
national professional institution that enters none of them. In both sports
the two simply exist side by side.

## The ritual as a condition

Thailand's place inside the federation is central. Thai federations made
[keeping the wai kru](#muaythai-wai-kru) a condition of taking the sport
abroad, on the argument that muaythai without it would not be muaythai. The
<Term id="wai-kru">wai kru ram muay</Term> is now written into the
competition rules, and a fighter must perform it. The federation also spells
the sport's name as one word, which is its own statement about who owns it.
