# What is still argued about

**The clock.** A <Term id="frame">frame</Term> of snooker can take an hour,
and a world championship final runs over two days. That is hard to schedule.
Snooker has kept its main event as it is and added shorter events with shot
clocks. Some professional pool uses shot clocks too. Players and fans in both
games argue about whether the short events are the future or a sideshow.

**The miss.** The rule asks a referee to judge what a player intended. It has
been rewritten many times and it still decides matches.

**Fixed matches.** Cue sports are played one against one, the matches are
long, and the betting on them is heavy. There have been match-fixing cases,
and professional snooker has suspended and banned players. Much of the
professional body's disciplinary system exists for this reason.

**Britain or China.** Snooker's growth and audience are increasingly in
China, and its institutions are still British. The question is being answered
slowly, by where the events are held.
