# Where it is played

Each of the three games has its own map, and the maps hardly overlap.

## Carom

Carom belongs to continental Europe, East Asia and Latin America. Belgium, the
Netherlands, Spain, Turkey, Sweden, South Korea, Japan, Vietnam, Colombia and
Mexico are its strong countries. Three-cushion draws large crowds in South
Korea and Vietnam. In English-speaking countries the game is almost unknown.

## Snooker

Snooker began among the British and its weight is moving to China. England
built the professional game and has most of its history. China now supplies a
large share of its growth and its audience, and more and more of its players.
Northern Ireland, Scotland, Wales, Australia, Thailand, India, Iran and
Belgium all produce players too.

## Pool

Pool is strongest in the United States, the Philippines, Chinese Taipei,
China, Germany, Poland and the United Kingdom. The Philippines has produced
world champions far out of proportion to its size.

## Why the maps differ

Each game took root where its table went. Carom tables travelled with
continental European trade and administration. Snooker tables followed the
British Army and the Commonwealth. Pool tables followed American commercial
culture into bars everywhere. No country is prominent in all three.
