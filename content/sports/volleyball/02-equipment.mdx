# The ball, and one shirt

Volleyball needs a ball and a net, and the rules about the ball have hardly
moved.

The laws fix the ball's circumference, weight and pressure. In the 2000s
manufacturers changed how it was built. Eighteen smooth panels became fewer,
dimpled ones, and a <Term id="float-serve">float serve</Term> behaved
measurably differently in the air. The specification did not have to change.

The one piece of clothing written into the rules is a shirt. The
<Term id="libero">libero</Term> must wear one that contrasts with the rest of
the team. The libero may not attack above the net, may not
<Term id="block">block</Term> and may not serve, and a referee can only apply
those limits if the player can be picked out at a glance.

Knee pads and sprung floors made the game safer. They arrived without a rule.
