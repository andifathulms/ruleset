# Where it is played

No single nation dominates volleyball. Brazil, Italy, Poland, the United
States, Russia, Serbia, France, Japan, China, Turkey and Iran have all been
serious international contenders within living memory.

<Photos ids="volleyball-kurek-2018 volleyball-iran-2016" />

One reason is how little the game needs. A court and a net fit in any sports
hall, and there is no expensive surface or equipment. Volleyball is a standard
part of school physical education across Europe, Latin America and East Asia.
Beach volleyball needs even less.

## Leagues, not state programmes

Poland and Brazil built long runs of success on domestic professional
leagues, where many nations rely on a centralised state programme.

<Photos ids="volleyball-poland-2019 volleyball-world-cup-2006" />

## The women's game

The women's map differs from the men's. China, the United States, Serbia and
Italy are strong, and women's volleyball has had professional structures for
a long time.

<Photos ids="volleyball-egonu volleyball-china-2016-celebration" />

## What an even map allows

Because no nation dominates, a rule change in volleyball is almost never read
as an attempt to hold one country back. A federation with an even competitive
map has more freedom to change its rules.
