# One name, two kinds of race

## Drafting

[In 1995 elite short-course duathlon became
draft-legal](#duathlon-drafting-formats), following
[triathlon](/sports/triathlon/). The reason given was that a ride without
<Term id="drafting">drafting</Term> spread the athletes far apart, and a race
like that could not be followed or shown on television.
<Term id="long-course">Long-course</Term> and amateur racing stayed
non-drafting.

<Photo id="duathlon-draft-legal-2011" />

The two are now different races under one name. In a draft-legal race a
strong cyclist gains much less than when riding alone, so the second run
decides the result. In a non-drafting race the ride is a time trial, and it
can decide the race by itself. The bicycles differ, the tactics differ, and a
time from one kind of race says nothing about a time from the other.

Some in the sport still ask whether elite duathlon needed to follow
triathlon. Duathlon is rarely on television under either rule.

## The name

For years many people called the sport biathlon. The winter sport of skiing
and shooting had the name first, and duathlon had to give it up. It took the
sport some years to settle on what it was called.
