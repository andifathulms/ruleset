# What is still argued about

**Whether elite racing should be draft-legal.** The rule was adopted to make
races close and suitable for television. Duathlon is rarely televised under
either rule, and a large part of the sport thinks a
<Term id="non-drafting">non-drafting</Term> elite race would be a truer test.

**How to police drafting.** Where drafting is banned, an official on a
motorcycle judges distance and time by eye. Automatic detection has been
proposed many times and is not in general use.

**Second place in its own federation.** Duathlon is governed by a body whose
Olympic sport is triathlon. Nothing proposed would change that, and it limits
how far duathlon can rise.

**Which distance is the sport.** The
<Term id="standard-distance">standard distance</Term>, the sprint, the long
course and <Term id="cross-duathlon">cross duathlon</Term> all have
championships. Nobody has settled which one stands for duathlon.

**Whether removing the swim removed the point.** Triathlon is defined by its
three disciplines. Duathlon's answer is that running, riding and running
again is a different test of the body, and that its second run is harder than
triathlon's only run. The argument is sound. It has not won duathlon a place
at the Games.
