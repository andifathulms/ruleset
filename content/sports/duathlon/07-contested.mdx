# What is contested now

Recorded as unsettled.

**Whether elite racing should be draft-legal.** The format was adopted
following triathlon, to make racing compressed and televisable. Duathlon is not
televised much either way, and a substantial part of the sport thinks a
non-drafting elite format would be a truer test. The split persists and the
argument recurs.

**Policing drafting.** In non-drafting racing the central call is made by an
official on a motorcycle judging distance and elapsed time. Automated detection
has been proposed repeatedly and is not in general use.

**Second place in its own federation.** Duathlon is governed by a body whose
Olympic sport is triathlon. That is a rational structure and it caps duathlon's
institutional ceiling permanently, and there is no proposal that would change
it.

**Distances.** Standard, sprint, long course and cross all have championships,
and which one represents the sport is unresolved — the same fragmentation
question this site records for several small federations.

**Whether removing the swim removed the point.** Triathlon's identity rests on
three disciplines. Duathlon's case is that the run-bike-run sequence is a
distinct physiological test rather than a reduced one, and that its second run
is harder than triathlon's only run. That argument is well founded and it has
never persuaded anyone with a programme place to give.
