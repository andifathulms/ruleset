# What is still argued about

**Drone racing's place.** It came from outside, and it is the one discipline
that is easy to watch. Its pilots have little in common with the gliding and
ballooning communities that built the FAI. How much weight it should carry is
disputed.

**Class boundaries.** Gliding's classes keep the contest between pilots.
Every boundary between two classes is an argument about which aircraft belong
together.

**Airspace.** National regulators balance commercial aviation, security and
defence. Recreational flying loses those arguments steadily, and no sporting
body has much influence over them.

**The edge of space.** The FAI ratifies records in aviation and in
spaceflight, and it fixed the accepted boundary of space at 100 kilometres.
Commercial spaceflight now has a commercial interest in where that line is.
Whether a sports federation should be the one to hold it is a question asked
mostly from outside the sport.
