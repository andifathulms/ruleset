# Where it is dived

Diving needs a deep pool and a tower. That makes it a sport of cities with
large aquatic centres, more than of small clubs.

The United States led for much of the twentieth century. Greg Louganis won
both men's events in 1984 and again in 1988. China won its first Olympic
diving gold in 1984 and has since become the standard. At Paris in 2024 it won
all eight events.

Mexico has a long diving tradition. Great Britain, Germany, Canada and
Australia win medals regularly.

## Beyond the Games

The world championships hold more events than the Olympics: the one-metre
springboard, mixed synchronised pairs and a mixed team event. Since 2013 they
have also held high diving, from 27 metres for men and 20 for women. None of
these is on the Olympic programme, which has stayed at eight events since
2000.

High diving is done from cliffs and from temporary towers. It has its own
circuit, and it grew up largely outside the federation before the world
championships took it in.
