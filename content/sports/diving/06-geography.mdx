# Where it is played

Diving needs a deep pool and a tower, which makes it a sport of cities with
Olympic-standard aquatic centres rather than of clubs.

The United States dominated it for much of the twentieth century; Greg
Louganis won both men's events in 1984 and again in 1988. China's
first Olympic diving gold came in 1984, and China has since become the
standard. At Paris in 2024 it won all eight events.

Mexico has a long diving tradition, and Great Britain, Germany, Canada and
Australia win regularly. The world championships and the World Cup are
where the rest of the world's divers meet China most often.

High diving, from cliffs and from temporary towers, has its own circuit, and
grew up largely outside the federation before the world championships took
it in.
