# Three episodes at the pool

## A world champion at twelve

Perth, 1991. Fu Mingxia of China won the world platform title at the age of
twelve. The diving authorities responded by setting [a minimum age of
fourteen](#fina-1992-minimum-age-fourteen) for the Games and the senior world
championships. Today the rule counts age at 31 December of the competition
year.

Fu still dived at Barcelona the following year. She turned fourteen twenty
days after the platform final, and she won gold.

## The head on the board

Seoul, 1988. In the preliminary round of the springboard, Greg Louganis struck
his head on the board. He was stitched, came back, and won the event. Then he
won the platform as well. In 1995 he said that he had known at the time that
he was HIV-positive. The episode changed no rule.

## The green pool

Rio de Janeiro, 2016. Midway through the Games, the diving pool at the Maria
Lenk Aquatics Centre turned green. The [water polo](/sports/water-polo/) pool
beside it followed. Officials blamed the water chemistry. No diving rule
changed, but for many people watching that summer it became the picture of
the sport.
