# Officiating

A referee runs the competition and the judges mark the dive. The referee
decides the matters of fact — whether the dive was the one announced,
whether a restart is due, whether the feet entered first — and can declare
a failed dive or cap the marks before the judges show them.

The judges award from 0 to 10 in half points, on their overall impression
of the approach, take-off, flight and entry. At the Games there are seven,
and the two highest and two lowest marks are cancelled. The remaining three
are added and multiplied by the degree of difficulty.

Synchronised diving needs eleven judges: three for each diver's execution
and five for how well the two match. Each group loses its highest and lowest
mark, and the remaining execution and synchronisation marks are averaged
into the same three-judge scale, so the score sits on the same footing as an
individual dive's.

What the judges never decide is how hard the dive was. That number is
printed in the table before the competition starts.
