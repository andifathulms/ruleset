# What is contested now

**How young is too young.** The minimum age is fourteen in the year of
competition, and the sport's champions are still often at the bottom of
that range. Quan Hongchan won the platform at Tokyo in 2021 at fourteen.

**High diving at the Games.** It has been on the world championship
programme since 2013 and is not on the Olympic one, including for 2028.

**The splash.** Entry is the hardest part of a dive to judge from the side
and the part judges weigh most visibly. Whether a panel of seven, trimmed
to three, measures it consistently is the sport's oldest open question.

**The table itself.** Because difficulty is computed, every change to the
formula's components changes which dives are worth attempting. The tables
are maintained by the technical diving committee, and divers plan their
lists around them.
