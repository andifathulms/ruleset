# What is still argued about

**How young is too young.** The minimum age is fourteen in the year of
competition, and champions are still often at the bottom of that range. Quan
Hongchan won the platform at Tokyo in 2021 at fourteen.

**High diving at the Games.** It has been on the world championship programme
since 2013. It is not on the Olympic one, including for 2028.

**The splash.** The entry is the hardest part of a dive to judge from the
side, and the part judges weigh most visibly. Whether seven judges, cut to
three, mark it consistently is the sport's oldest open question.

**The table itself.** Because difficulty is computed, every change to the
values in the formula changes which dives are worth attempting. Divers plan
their lists around the tables.
