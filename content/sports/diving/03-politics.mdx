# Governing body politics

Diving has been governed with swimming since FINA was founded in London in
1908, and the federation has been called World Aquatics since 2023. The
diving rules are written by its technical diving committee, which also
maintains the difficulty tables.

Two of the sport's largest changes came from outside that committee. The
minimum age was set after a twelve-year-old won a world title, and the
synchronised events joined the Games in 2000 under the IOC's authority, by
Olympedia's account at television's request. Neither was a technical
reform of how diving is judged.

The world championships carry far more events than the Games. They hold the
one-metre springboard, mixed synchronised pairs and a mixed team event, and
since 2013 high diving from 27 metres for men and 20 for women. None of these
is on the Olympic programme, which has held at eight events since 2000.
