# What is still argued about

**Prevention or medals.** The federation says the sport exists to support its
work against drowning. How much money should go to elite competition, and how
much to teaching in the countries where people drown, is a live argument
inside it.

**Pool or ocean.** There have been two programmes in one federation since
1993. The pool events travel and appear at the World Games. The ocean events
are the older competitive culture and need a surf beach. Which of them is the
real sport has not been settled.

**Speed against rescue.** Technique must follow real rescue practice. That
rule is the base of the sport, and it rubs against every clock. The line moves
each time rescue guidance changes.

**Beach flags.** It has the highest injury rate of any event, in a sport that
exists to prevent harm. The rules on the start and on contact have been
adjusted, and the problem remains.

**Times that are not records.** Surf, current and sandbanks differ from one
heat to the next, so an ocean result depends partly on the draw. The sport
records the times and does not call them records.
[Rowing](/sports/rowing/) takes the same position. It is honest, and it
leaves lifesaving without a list of records to show how it has progressed.
