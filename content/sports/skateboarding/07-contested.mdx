# What is still argued about

**Whether impression judging can last.** Judging without a table is hard to
keep consistent, and the criteria were revised between the sport's first two
Games. The other choice is to write a table of trick values, and skaters
reject that.

**National programmes.** Selection, funding and coaching for young skaters now
exist where there were none. Many in the community see what has been gained
and still count it as a loss.

**Age.** Top-level street has produced medallists in their early teens, and
injuries in skateboarding build up over years. Whether the contest structure
suits competitors that young is an open question.

**Vert.** The half-pipe is outside the Olympic programme, and some skaters
keep arguing for its return beside street and <Term id="park">park</Term>.

**Who is in charge.** World Skate's authority comes from Olympic recognition.
The commercial circuit is decades older and carries on beside it. Which of the
two is the sport's real competition is not settled.
