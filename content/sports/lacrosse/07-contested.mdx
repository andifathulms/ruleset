# What is contested now

Recorded as unsettled.

**Whether Sixes is lacrosse.** The Olympic format did not exist before 2021,
has no specialist positions, no long sticks and no face-off, and produces a
much higher-scoring game than field lacrosse. Supporters say it is the version
that can globalise the sport. Opponents say the Games will showcase something
that most lacrosse players do not play, which is the same objection cricket
faces about Twenty20 and rugby faces about sevens.

**Haudenosaunee participation beyond 2028.** The 2025 accommodation covers the
Los Angeles Games. Whether it establishes a precedent, and what happens to a
team that is not a state at future Games, is unresolved.

**Headgear in the women's game.** Permitted since 2017, not mandated, and the
argument continues in both directions — that optional protection creates a
two-tier risk profile, and that mandating it would change how the game is
played.

**Four formats.** Men's field, women's field, box and Sixes have different
rules, different equipment and different statistics. A sport this size
supporting four versions of itself is a structural question the federation has
not addressed, and the Olympic decision has made one of them dominant without
resolving anything about the others.
