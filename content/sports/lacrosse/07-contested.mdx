# What is still argued about

**Is Sixes lacrosse?** The Olympic format has no specialist positions, no long
sticks and no <Term id="face-off">face-off</Term> after a goal, and it
produces many more goals than field lacrosse. Its supporters say it is the
form that can take the game around the world. Its critics say the Games will
show a game that most lacrosse players do not play.
[Cricket](/sports/cricket/) hears the same complaint about Twenty20, and
[rugby](/sports/rugby/) about sevens.

**The Haudenosaunee after 2028.** The 2025 arrangement covers the Los Angeles
Games. Nobody has said whether it sets a precedent, or what happens at later
Games to a team that does not represent a state.

**Headgear in the women's game.** It has been allowed since 2017 and is not
required. Some argue that optional protection leaves players at two levels of
risk. Others argue that making it compulsory would change how the game is
played.

**Four formats.** Men's field, women's field, box and Sixes have different
rules, different equipment and statistics that cannot be compared. The Olympic
decision has made one of the four the most visible. It has settled nothing
about the other three.
