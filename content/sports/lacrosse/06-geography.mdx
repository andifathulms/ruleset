# Where it is played

Lacrosse is strongest in the United States and Canada. In Canada the main
form is <Term id="box-lacrosse">box lacrosse</Term>, played indoors on a rink.
The Haudenosaunee Nationals are regularly among the top three teams in the
world. They represent a confederacy of territories with fewer people than most
cities.

<Photo id="lacrosse-six-nations-2011" />

Outside North America the game is played in England, Australia and Japan, and
by a growing group that includes Israel, Ireland and several other European
nations.

## Why the map stayed small

Field lacrosse needs ten players a side, specialist positions and costly
protective equipment. A country needs clubs to produce such players, and clubs
exist only where the game is already played. That circle kept the map almost
unchanged for a century.

Sixes was written to break it. With six players, no specialists, no long
sticks, a smaller field and shorter matches, a national federation can build a
competitive team without a deep club system. The 2028 Games will be the first
large test of whether that works.
