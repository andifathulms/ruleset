# Where it is played

Fifteens belongs to a small, stable and wealthy group of nations. New Zealand,
South Africa, France, England, Ireland, Australia, Wales, Scotland, Italy and
Argentina lead it. Japan and Georgia are the strongest recent arrivals.

## What sevens changed

<Term id="sevens">Sevens</Term> has a wider map. Fiji, a country of under a
million people, is a leading nation. Its first Olympic gold medal in any sport
came in rugby sevens in 2016, and its second came in the men's sevens at the
Tokyo Games. Kenya, Samoa, Tonga and Spain compete in sevens in a way they do
not in fifteens.

<Photos ids="rugby-fiji-fans rugby-tokyo-sevens" />

The reason is in the format. A sevens team needs seven players, where a
fifteens squad needs thirty with specialists for the
<Term id="scrum">scrum</Term> and the <Term id="lineout">lineout</Term>. A
tournament takes three days where fifteens takes a month. The skills are
running and handling, which a smaller union can teach without a professional
club system.

## The women's game

Women were playing rugby in Cardiff in 1917. Over the last decade the women's
game has grown faster than any other part of rugby. Its strongest nations are
New Zealand, England, France, Canada and Australia.

## Rugby league

Rugby league has a narrower map: northern England, eastern Australia, New
Zealand, Papua New Guinea and France. It is close to a map of where the split
