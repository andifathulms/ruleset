# Where it is played

Ultimate is strongest in North America, northern Europe, Japan, Colombia and
Australia.

The United States and Canada have the deepest structures. College ultimate
there produces more players than any other system in the world, and North
America has the only large professional leagues. Japan has been among the
best for decades. In Europe the strong national teams are Great Britain,
Germany, Finland, Sweden, Austria and Switzerland. An indoor game with five
players a side keeps ultimate going through the northern winter.

## Colombia

Colombia had no tradition of ultimate, no professional league and very little
money. It became a world power, above all in the mixed and women's divisions,
through a programme in schools and communities. The equipment is one disc and
the officials are the players, so it costs a country very little to begin.
Colombia shows what can be done with that.

## Disc golf

Disc golf has a different map, and a bigger one. It is a mass sport in the
United States and in Finland, and it grew sharply in the early 2020s. Almost
none of those players appear in the multi-sport events where the federation
does its work. Very many people throw a disc, and the disc sports have little
official standing to show for it.
