# Two arguments on the field

## The count

A thrower has ten seconds to release the disc. The clock is the voice of the
<Term id="marker">marker</Term>, who counts aloud. The marker is an opponent
and gains from counting fast. Disputes about a fast
<Term id="stall-count">stall count</Term> are routine. The two players
involved settle them, like every other dispute in the game.

## Contact at speed

Ultimate forbids contact. It is also played by athletes who sprint towards
the same disc while it is in the air, and collisions happen anyway. They
happen most on a <Term id="bid">bid</Term>, when a player dives for the disc.

<Photo id="flying-disc-bid-aachen" />

What counts as a dangerous play? Is the player who was moving fastest the one
responsible? The rules on this have been rewritten several times. It is the
main safety question the sport has.
