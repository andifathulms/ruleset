# What is still argued about

**The Olympic Games.** The federation was recognised by the International
Olympic Committee in 2015, and none of its sports has been added to the
programme. Ultimate has been played at the World Games, the event for sports
that are not Olympic, since the early 2000s.

<Photo id="flying-disc-world-games-2005" />

A sport that wants a place at the Olympics is expected to settle its matches
clearly. In ultimate a dispute the players cannot settle sends the disc back
to the thrower, and a final decided that way is hard to broadcast. The other
obstacles are a game played to a score with no clock, and a mixed division as
a main event. The sport does not want to give up any of the three. The real
question is whether a place at the Games is worth the cost.

**The mixed division.** Ultimate has open, women's and mixed divisions, and
all three are full championships. Very few sports treat a mixed event that
way. People in the sport argue about whether mixed should be its leading
division. The answer matters if ultimate is ever proposed for a programme
that counts medal events.

**Disc golf.** Far more people play disc golf than ultimate. It has its own
professional tour and its own equipment industry. It has almost no part in
the multi-sport events the federation has worked inside for forty years. If
the federation's largest sport is not the one it campaigns with, people ask
what the federation is for.
