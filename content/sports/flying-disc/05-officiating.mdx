# A game with no referee

Ultimate is played without a referee at every level, including the world
championship.

## How a call works

A player who is fouled calls "<Term id="foul">foul</Term>" aloud, during
play, and the game stops. The player accused may disagree. If the two cannot
agree, the disc goes back to the thrower, and the game starts again from
there as if the pass had not been thrown. Nothing is awarded to either side.

<Term id="spirit">Spirit of the Game</Term> is the first clause of the
rulebook, and every other rule assumes that no official is present. It asks
the players to know the rules, to apply them honestly against themselves, and
never to break them on purpose. In most sports a deliberate foul is a tactic
with a price. In ultimate it is an offence against the game.

## When the stakes rise

Calling your own fouls works well when the players share a culture and
little is at stake. A world final is different. A player with a professional
contract or a national programme behind them must make a call against their
own interest, in front of a crowd. A televised final decided by a dispute
that two players could not settle was hard for the sport to defend.

## Observers

In 2014 [the federation introduced a game advisor, and top events began to
use observers](#disc-observers-and-referees). An
<Term id="observer">observer</Term> is a neutral official. An observer
settles a dispute that the players cannot. An observer rules on whether a
disc was in or out and whether the stall count was fair, keeps the time
limits, and may penalise misconduct or send a player off. An observer does
not call fouls. That still belongs to the players.

The federation says that observers support the players' own officiating.
Critics inside the sport say that an official who can overrule a disputed
call and send a player off is a referee under another name. Whether observers
are a lasting compromise or a step on the way to referees is argued in public
by people in the sport.

## Leagues with referees

Some professional leagues, mostly in North America, went all the way and
appointed referees. That made the game easier to show to a crowd, and it
removed the rule the game was built around.

The same players also play for their national teams under the federation's
rules, with no referee. So the most visible league and the world championship
are officiated in two different ways.
