# Where it is played

Water polo is strongest in central and southern Europe, and above all in
Hungary. Hungarian men have won nine Olympic gold medals, the first in 1932
and the ninth in 2008. They won a medal at every Games from 1928 to 1980. The
sport is part of national life there in a way it is almost nowhere else.

The Balkans are the second centre. Yugoslavia won three Olympic titles, and
the countries that followed it have kept the strength. Serbia won the men's
gold in 2016, 2020 and 2024. Croatia and Montenegro are contenders every
time. Italy and Spain complete the European core.

## The United States

The American men's only Olympic title dates from 1904. The American women
have led the women's game, with gold in 2012, 2016 and 2020. Australia won the
first women's title in 2000, the Netherlands won in 2008 and Spain in 2024.

## Why so few countries

Water polo needs a deep pool that is given over to it for hours at a time.
That limits it to places with many pools. It also grows through clubs, and a
club system takes decades to build. Where those clubs exist the sport is
professional and deep. Elsewhere it is a minor sport for swimmers, who must
already be strong in the water before they can begin.
