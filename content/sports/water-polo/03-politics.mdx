# One rule package, made two ways

Water polo is run by World Aquatics, a federation whose main business is
swimming. In six years it changed the rules of water polo by two very
different routes.

## Hangzhou, 2018

The first route was slow. A set of changes was proposed at a water polo
congress in Budapest in April 2018. It was tried at youth and World Cup
events. USA Water Polo reported that the trials produced 25 per cent more
goals and 24 per cent fewer fouls called per game. In December 2018 an
extraordinary congress in Hangzhou [adopted the package by 161 votes to
2](#fina-2019-twenty-second-reset).

## A postal vote, 2024

The second route was fast. In October 2024 the technical committee
recommended a new package. The men's field would shrink from 30 metres to 25,
the size the women already used. The <Term id="possession-clock">possession
clock</Term> would drop to 25 seconds and the
<Term id="exclusion">exclusion</Term> to 15. Coaches would get a video
challenge. The package had been presented as a set of trials for the 2025
World Cup. In November the federation's Bureau [adopted it outright by postal
vote](#wa-2024-twenty-five-metre-field), with no congress and before any
trial.

Leading coaches objected, and more to the method than to the content. Dejan
Savić, the former coach of Serbia, called the process "devastating" and the
challenge "a farce".

## Six months later

The 2025 World Cup was played under the new rules. In May 2025 the federation
[went halfway back](#wa-2025-twenty-eight-eighteen): 28 seconds to shoot, and
18 for an exclusion. Its letter to the national federations said the changes
"seek to improve the overall speed and efficiency of the sport, while also
enhancing the player safety and overall fairness at all levels of
competition".

Coaches are still divided about the game that came out of it. Ratko Rudić
said it had "lost much of the tactical component". Tom Hoad called it "more
television appropriate now".

The shorter field stayed. The same seven players now cover a sixth less
water, and every attack starts closer to goal. Water polo keeps no records
that a shorter pool could spoil. What changed was the shape of the game.

## Twelve women's teams

Women's water polo was played at world championships from 1986 and entered
the Olympics in 2000 with six teams. In April 2025 the International Olympic
Committee [agreed a twelve-team women's tournament for
2028](#ioc-2025-women-twelve-teams). It will be the same size as the men's
for the first time.
