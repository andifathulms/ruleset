# What is contested now

Recorded as unsettled.

**How rules get made.** The 2019 package was trialled and voted at a
congress; the 2024 package was adopted by postal vote and partly reversed six
months later. The federation has set up a rules revision group, and whether
future changes go back to trial-and-congress is the open question underneath
all the others.

**The 25-metre men's field.** Supporters say it produces more shots, less dead
time and a game more suited to television. Opponents say it removes tactics
and produces lopsided scores. Both positions are put by people who have
coached at the highest level.

**The clocks.** Possession at 28 seconds and exclusion at 18 are compromises
between the old 30 and 20 and the short-lived 25 and 15. There is no reason to
think they are the final numbers.

**Seeing the game.** Video review and the Coach's Challenge have helped with
goals and violence. They cannot see underwater, and the sport's central call —
which hold was legal — remains a judgement made from above.

**Parity.** From 2028 the women's Olympic tournament is the same size as the
men's, twenty-eight years after it was first held.
