# What is still argued about

**How rules get made.** One package was tried out and voted on at a congress.
The next was adopted by postal vote and partly reversed six months later. The
federation has set up a group to revise the rules. Whether future changes go
back to trials and a congress is the question under all the others.

**The 25-metre men's field.** Supporters say it brings more shots, less dead
time and a game better suited to television. Opponents say it removes tactics
and produces lopsided scores. Both views come from people who have coached at
the highest level.

**The clocks.** Twenty-eight seconds to shoot and eighteen for an
<Term id="exclusion">exclusion</Term> are compromises between the old 30 and
20 and the short-lived 25 and 15. Nothing suggests they are the final
numbers.

**Seeing the game.** Video review and the coach's challenge have helped with
goals and with violence. They cannot see under the water. Which hold was
legal is still judged from above.
