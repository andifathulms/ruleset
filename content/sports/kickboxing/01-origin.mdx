# Karate at full force

Kickboxing began with karate fighters who wanted to hit each other properly.

Competitive karate scored controlled techniques and stopped the bout to award
each one. Some practitioners wanted more. Through the 1960s and 1970s, in the
United States, Japan and Europe, they built formats in which strikes were
thrown at full force and the bout did not stop. The three places worked more
or less independently. The American version was called full contact karate
before the name changed.

<Photo id="kickboxing-japan-1969" />

Each version borrowed from [boxing](/sports/boxing/), some more and some
less. The <Term id="ring">ring</Term>, gloves, rounds, weight categories and
the <Term id="ten-point-must">ten-point must system</Term> all came across.
Kicks were added on top.

## The fighters who stopped kicking

The borrowing caused a problem almost at once. A kick is slower than a punch,
more tiring, and easier to counter. A fighter who only wants to win has a
reason to stop kicking. Fighters with boxing backgrounds neglected kicks
completely, and early full-contact bouts drifted towards boxing in heavier
gloves.

The answer is still in the rulebook. [In full contact a fighter must throw a
minimum number of kicks in every round](#kickboxing-minimum-kicks), commonly
six, or be warned and then lose points.

## One federation, many codes

[WAKO was formed in 1976](#kickboxing-1976-wako) to bring the national
versions together, with weight categories, rounds and a written rulebook. It
never made them into one sport. From the beginning it has governed a family
of codes: <Term id="full-contact">full contact</Term>,
<Term id="low-kick">low kick</Term>, <Term id="k1-style">K-1 style</Term>,
<Term id="point-fighting">point fighting</Term>, light contact, kick light,
and <Term id="musical-forms">musical forms</Term>.

<Photo id="kickboxing-codes-2019" />

The International Olympic Committee gave WAKO provisional recognition in 2018
and [full recognition in 2021](#kickboxing-2021-ioc-recognition). Kickboxing
has been at the World Games since 2022. It has never been at the Olympic
Games.
