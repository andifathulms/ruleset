# What is contested now

Recorded as unsettled.

**Which federation speaks for the sport.** IOC recognition went to WAKO in
2021. Rival international bodies continue to sanction world titles, and
commercial promotions run their own rules where most of the money is. Nothing
reconciles them.

**Which discipline is kickboxing.** Full contact bars leg kicks, low kick
permits them, K-1 style adds knees, and point fighting stops the bout after
every point. Any Olympic proposition would have to pick one, and picking would
exclude most of the federation's own athletes.

**The minimum kick rule.** It works, and it is an admission. A sport that must
require its competitors to use its defining technique invites the question of
whether the technique is competitively viable on its own terms, and that
question gets asked whenever the rule is enforced against a fighter who was
winning.

**Judging.** Continuous bouts are scored by eye with the difficulties boxing
has had for a century. Boxing tried electronic scoring in 1992 and abandoned it
in 2016; taekwondo instrumented its scoring and changed the sport. Kickboxing
has done neither and has the same exposure.

**Headguards.** Kept in the light codes and variable in the ring ones, while
boxing removed them for senior men in 2016 on evidence the sport reads
differently.

**Olympic prospects.** Recognition since 2021, no place, and a sport whose
internal disagreements about its own rules are the first thing any assessment
would encounter.
