# Where it is fought

Kickboxing is strongest in Europe as a competition, in Japan as a business,
and everywhere as exercise.

## Europe

WAKO's competitive strength is in Europe. Italy, Russia, Ukraine, Hungary,
Greece, Serbia, Croatia, Germany, England and Ireland all send deep squads.
The federation was founded in Europe and has always had its headquarters
there. Its championships are largely European events with entrants from the
rest of the world.

## Japan

Japan produced the commercial version that most people have seen. Japanese
promotions built the ring format that gave
<Term id="k1-style">K-1 style</Term> its name, and for a time they were among
the largest properties in combat sport. So a discipline in an amateur
federation's rulebook carries the name of a promotion.

## The gym

As recreation, kickboxing is enormous, and it has almost no connection to any
of this. Bag and pad classes are standard in commercial gyms around the
world. Most people who say they do kickboxing have never entered a
competition and do not train in any of the six disciplines.

<Photos ids="kickboxing-fitness-naples kickboxing-fitness-ramstein" />

Very many people take part, while the competitive sport is concentrated in
one region and divided among rival bodies.
[Muaythai](/sports/muaythai/) has the same shape, but it has one clear summit
for its best fighters. Kickboxing has none.
