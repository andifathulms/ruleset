# Four moments that changed the game

## The pad

Batters had found a safe answer to a ball outside the off stump. They pushed
a padded leg at it and took no risk with the bat. It was legal and it worked,
and it made long parts of a match dull.

<Photos ids="cricket-lbw-1904 cricket-lbw-1947" />

[The 1980 Code rewrote the law](#mcc-1980-code-lbw) on
<Term id="lbw">leg before wicket</Term>. A batter can now be out to a ball
that lands outside the off stump, if the batter made no real attempt to play
it with the bat.

## Melbourne, January 1971

Rain washed out a <Term id="test">Test match</Term> in Melbourne. The
organisers did not want to send the crowd home with nothing, so they arranged
a one-day game on what would have been the last day. Forty-six thousand
people came.

That improvised game was the first
<Term id="one-day">one-day international</Term>. [The format was adopted on
purpose afterwards](#icc-1971-limited-overs), with a fixed number of
<Term id="over">overs</Term> for each side and a result in a day. It did not
replace the five-day game. Cricket began to keep two sets of records.

## An evening game, 2003

Crowds at domestic matches in England had been falling for years.
[Twenty20](#icc-2003-twenty20) was designed to fit into the length of a
working evening: twenty overs a side, finished inside three hours. It began
as a domestic competition in England in 2003 and was played between countries
within two years. It has been a great commercial success.

<Photo id="cricket-world-t20-2007" />

It also gave cricket a third set of records. A batting average or a scoring
rate means something different in each format. The sport knew this from the
start, and it has never merged the three.

## Lord's, 14 July 2019

The World Cup final ended level. The <Term id="super-over">Super Over</Term>
played to settle it ended level too. England were given the title because
they had hit more boundaries in the match.

Almost nobody thought that was a fair way to decide a world championship.
Within months [the rule was removed](#icc-2019-boundary-countback-removed).
A tied Super Over is now followed by another, until one side wins.
