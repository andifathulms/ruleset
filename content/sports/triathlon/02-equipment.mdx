# Two bicycles for one sport

Triathlon uses two sets of equipment at the same time, one for professionals
and one for everybody else.

<Term id="elite">Elite</Term> athletes are allowed to ride close behind each
other, so they ride in a <Term id="pack">pack</Term>.
<Term id="tri-bars">Tri-bars</Term> are forbidden there, because a rider
resting on them in a pack cannot brake or steer well enough. Elite
triathletes ride ordinary road bicycles in a bunch, and a teammate may work
for a leader as in a [cycling](/sports/cycling/) road race.

<Photos ids="triathlon-chasing-pack-2012 triathlon-brownlee-hayes-2012" />

<Term id="age-group">Age-group</Term> athletes must ride alone. Tri-bars are
allowed, and so the equipment contest is about moving through the air: deep
wheels, smooth helmets, tri-bars and time-trial frames. The equipment most
triathletes own is equipment the professionals may not use.

## The wetsuit

The water temperature decides the <Term id="wetsuit">wetsuit</Term>. Below
one temperature it must be worn. Above another it is banned. A wetsuit keeps
a swimmer warm, and it also helps the body float, which makes the swimmer
faster.

## The helmet

The helmet must be fastened before the bicycle is lifted from the
<Term id="rack">rack</Term>, and it must stay fastened until the bicycle is
back on it. A safety rule has become a step that is done against the clock.
It is enforced strictly, and it has decided races.
