# Where it is raced

Triathlon is strongest in wealthy countries where children learn to swim.

Great Britain, France, Australia, New Zealand, the United States, Germany,
Spain, Switzerland and Norway win most often. They share a swimming culture,
roads that are good for cycling, and many amateurs with the money for three
sports' equipment and the time to train for them.

<Photos ids="triathlon-jorgensen-2013 triathlon-duffy-2016" />

There are exceptions. Flora Duffy won Bermuda's first Olympic gold medal in
2021.

The swim limits where the sport can grow, as it does for
[swimming](/sports/swimming/) itself. A country without pools that people can
reach, and without the habit of teaching children to swim, cannot produce
triathletes. Talent for running or cycling does not replace it. Recent growth
in Latin America and East Asia has followed the building of pools.

## The amateurs pay for it

Triathlon has a very large number of amateur competitors for the size of its
professional field. Their entry fees and equipment pay for the sport. They
also race under the older rule, with no
<Term id="drafting">drafting</Term>. The part of the sport that earns the
money and the part shown at the Olympics follow different rules.

<Photos ids="triathlon-age-group-swim triathlon-dorney-transition" />

The long races that most of the public knows are run by commercial
organisers, and the governing body does not run them. In this triathlon
resembles [golf](/sports/golf/) and [tennis](/sports/tennis/) more than most
Olympic sports.
