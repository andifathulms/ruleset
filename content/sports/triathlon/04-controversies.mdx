# The decision that made a pack race

## Drafting, 1995

The largest change in triathlon's rules came from a decision, with no single
incident behind it.

Until 1995 <Term id="drafting">drafting</Term> was a penalty offence for
everyone. Officials on motorcycles had to judge the gap between moving
bicycles over a 40 kilometre course, again and again. It was expensive and
inconsistent, and races were regularly decided by a penalty that somebody
disputed.

[In 1995 the federation made drafting legal in elite
racing](#itu-1995-drafting-legalised). The problem of judging the gap was
gone. The riders formed packs and reached the last
<Term id="transition">transition</Term> together, which made a far better
race for television than riders arriving one by one.

<Photos ids="triathlon-tv-motorbike-2012 triathlon-hyde-park-bike-2012" />

It also meant the ride stopped separating the athletes. Competitors and
coaches said at the time that a triathlon in which one of the three
disciplines decides nothing does not test three disciplines. They still say
it, and the federation has not disputed the description. An Olympic triathlon
is now usually won by the fastest runner in the front pack. At Paris in 2024
Alex Yee of Great Britain took the gold medal in the final kilometre of the
run.

## Two rulebooks

[The federation kept the old rule for age-group
racing](#worldtri-age-group-no-drafting). Its reason was safety: packs of
inexperienced riders at speed are dangerous, and a mass field cannot be
watched as an elite race can.

So triathlon has had two versions of its central rule for thirty years.
Almost everyone who races is in a different sport from the one they watch,
with different rules and different bicycles.
[Baseball](/sports/baseball/) ran two batting rules in its two leagues for
forty-nine years, and that split was famous. This one divides the
professionals from everybody else, and few people remark on it.

## The water

The swim is held in rivers, harbours and the sea, in water the organiser does
not control. After heavy rain the water can carry too many bacteria to be
safe. Swims at several major events have been shortened, postponed or
cancelled, and the swims at the Paris 2024 Games were in doubt for that
reason.

<Photos ids="triathlon-seine-buoys triathlon-seine-swim-safety" />

[The rules now set limits for water quality](#worldtri-water-quality). If the
tests fail, the swim is shortened or removed, and the race becomes a
<Term id="duathlon">duathlon</Term>. The sport can measure the water. It
cannot clean it. A result in triathlon's own records may therefore describe a
race with only two disciplines.
